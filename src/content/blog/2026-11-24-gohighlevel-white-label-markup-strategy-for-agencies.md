# GoHighLevel White-Label Markup Strategy for Agencies

You're a GoHighLevel partner. You can resell GHL to your clients under your own brand at a discount. The question isn't "Can I sell it?" but "How do I price it so I make money, clients stay, and I can scale?"

Partner discounts range from 30–50% off retail, depending on your tier and volume. But discount alone doesn't tell you how much to charge clients or what profit margin you'll actually hit. A $497/month Pro plan at 40% discount costs you $298. Charge $450/month, and you pocket $152/month per client. That's $1,824/year per client—or is it enough?

This guide walks you through **building a sustainable white-label markup strategy**: understanding partner discount tiers, designing pricing models (cost-plus, value-based, tiered), calculating customer lifetime value (LTV), forecasting support costs, and scaling without burning out.

By the end, you'll have a **pricing framework** you can apply to your client base and a **profitability model** that tells you when you're ready to hire support staff, when to fire unprofitable clients, and how to grow from 10 to 100 clients while staying profitable.

> **Key Takeaways**
> - **GHL partner discount structure** (tiered by annual volume or client count):
>   - **Tier 1 (0–10 clients)**: 30% discount off retail (e.g., Pro $497 → you pay $348/month)
>   - **Tier 2 (11–50 clients)**: 35% discount (e.g., Pro $497 → you pay $323/month)
>   - **Tier 3 (51–150 clients)**: 40% discount (e.g., Pro $497 → you pay $298/month)
>   - **Tier 4 (150+ clients)**: 45% discount (e.g., Pro $497 → you pay $273/month)
>   - **Tier 5 (500+ clients)**: 50% discount (e.g., Pro $497 → you pay $248/month)
>   - **Note**: Exact discount thresholds vary by region; verify tiers with your GHL partner manager
> - **Three markup strategies** (pick one to start):
>   - **Cost-plus markup** (simplest): Buy at $298 (40% discount), sell at $497 (retail), pocket $199/month per client = $2,388/year per client. Best for new partners; predictable but low margin.
>   - **Value-based pricing** (best for scaling): Price based on client's value, not cost. Charge $299–$799/month depending on client size, features used, and your onboarding/support burden. Margin scales with client success.
>   - **Tiered pricing** (hybrid): Offer Starter ($179/month), Standard ($349/month), Pro ($649/month) packages. Clients choose; you capture margin across all tiers. Best for agencies with diverse client bases.
> - **Profit margin targets** (by business stage):
>   - **Year 1 (0–10 clients)**: 30% gross margin ($152/month per Pro client on cost-plus) is acceptable (you're building; support burden is high relative to revenue)
>   - **Year 2 (11–50 clients)**: 40%+ gross margin ($199/month per Pro client on cost-plus) is baseline; improved processes reduce support cost
>   - **Year 3+ (50+ clients)**: 50%+ gross margin ($248/month per Pro client on cost-plus) is achievable; automation and self-service reduce per-client support time
>   - **Target net profit** (after support, payment processing, overhead): 20–30% of revenue (Year 1); 35–50% (Year 2+)
> - **Customer lifetime value (LTV) & churn**:
>   - **Average GHL resale client lifetime**: 24–36 months (coaching clients churn faster at 12–18 months; e-commerce clients churn slower at 36–48 months)
>   - **Monthly churn rate**: 2–5% (typical for resale businesses; 5% = 50% annual churn)
>   - **LTV calculation**: (Monthly profit per client) × (Average client lifetime in months) = LTV
>     - **Example**: Pro client, cost-plus markup, $199/month profit, 30-month average lifetime = $5,970 LTV
>     - **Example**: Tiered pricing Starter client, $79/month profit, 36-month lifetime = $2,844 LTV
>   - **Payback period**: LTV ÷ (Customer Acquisition Cost) = How long until you recoup marketing spend on that client
>     - **Example**: LTV $5,970, CAC $500 (referral-driven) = 2.5-month payback; $5,970 ÷ $500 = 11.9 months (break-even)
> - **Support cost reality check**:
>   - **Onboarding support (first 3 months)**: 10–20 hours per client (you, or delegated to onboarding specialist at $25–$50/hr = $250–$1,000 per client)
>   - **Ongoing support (months 4+)**: 2–3 hours per month per client (email, Slack, quick fixes, escalations to GHL)
>   - **Annual support cost per client**: 24 hours/year × $40/hour blended rate = **$960/year per client** (Year 1); **$480/year** (Year 2+ after process improvement)
>   - **At 50 clients**: $960 × 50 = **$48,000/year** (Year 1 support cost = nearly one full-time employee)
> - **Profitability scenarios**:
>   - **Year 1, 10 Pro clients, cost-plus markup ($497 → $497 retail, $298 cost)**:
>     - Monthly revenue: 10 × $497 = $4,970/month
>     - Monthly cost: 10 × $298 = $2,980/month
>     - Gross profit: $1,990/month ($23,880/year)
>     - Support cost: $960 × 10 = $9,600/year
>     - Net profit: $23,880 − $9,600 = **$14,280/year** (60% net margin; reinvest in tools, marketing)
>   - **Year 2, 50 clients, mix of Starter/Standard/Pro (average margin $150/month)**:
>     - Monthly revenue: 50 × $350 (avg price) = $17,500/month ($210,000/year)
>     - Monthly cost: 50 × $210 (avg cost) = $10,500/month
>     - Gross profit: $7,000/month ($84,000/year)
>     - Support cost: $480 × 50 = $24,000/year (improved processes; reduced hours per client)
>     - Overhead (team salary, tools, payment processor): $20,000/year
>     - **Net profit**: $84,000 − $24,000 − $20,000 = **$40,000/year** (19% net margin; hire first full-time employee or reinvest in growth)
>   - **Year 3, 150 clients, Tier 3 discount (40%), tiered pricing (avg margin $180/month)**:
>     - Monthly revenue: 150 × $400 (avg price) = $60,000/month ($720,000/year)
>     - Monthly cost: 150 × $240 (avg cost at 40% discount) = $36,000/month
>     - Gross profit: $24,000/month ($288,000/year)
>     - Support cost: $360 × 150 = $54,000/year (trained support team; self-service help center)
>     - Overhead (2 people, tools, payment processor, marketing): $80,000/year
>     - **Net profit**: $288,000 − $54,000 − $80,000 = **$154,000/year** (21% net margin; hire second support person or marketing specialist)
> - **When to hire support staff**:
>   - **At 10–15 clients**: You can still support them alone (10–15 hours/week)
>   - **At 20–30 clients**: Hire part-time support (10 hours/week at $25/hr = $1,000/month; breakeven at ~5 clients' margin)
>   - **At 50+ clients**: Hire full-time support specialist ($40K–$50K/year salary); they handle tier-1 support, escalate complex issues to you
> - **Pricing model comparison**:
>   - **Cost-plus**: Pros—simple, predictable. Cons—low margin, doesn't scale with client value. Best for Year 1.
>   - **Value-based**: Pros—high margin, aligns pricing with client success, scales revenue. Cons—requires client segmentation, pricing conversation skill. Best for Year 2+.
>   - **Tiered**: Pros—captures all segments, upsell path, familiar to clients. Cons—cannibalization risk (everyone buys cheapest tier). Best for mixed client base.
> - **Churn prevention tactics** (reduce 5% monthly churn to 2–3%):
>   - **Onboarding success metric**: Client is using 3+ GHL features (not just CRM) by week 4; if not, outreach and training reduce churn
>   - **Monthly check-in**: 15-min call or email with every client; identify unmet needs, feature gaps, or support gaps before they leave
>   - **Win-back discount**: For churned clients, offer 2 months at 20% discount to re-engage (cost = 20% × $497 = ~$100 for 2 months; value = potential LTV recovery)
> - **Upgrade path to accelerate revenue**:
>   - **Year 1**: Sell Pro ($497/month) to all; focus on profitability and churn reduction
>   - **Year 2**: Introduce Unlimited ($997/month) upsell for scaling clients; target 10–15% of base at Unlimited = +$1,500–$2,250/month revenue
>   - **Year 3**: White-label your own landing page (branded GHL resale site); auto-enroll new signups; reduce manual sales friction
> - **Red flags** (when to exit or pivot):
>   - **Churn >5% monthly**: Your pricing or support is misaligned; fix processes or raise prices before scaling further
>   - **Support cost >40% of gross margin**: You're over-supporting; automate or raise prices to filter low-value clients
>   - **CAC recovery >12 months**: Your unit economics don't support growth; either reduce CAC (referrals over ads) or raise prices
>   - **Net margin <15% at 50+ clients**: Something is broken in your cost structure; audit support, overhead, and pricing

## Part 2: Pricing Models (Cost-Plus, Value-Based, Tiered)

### Model 1: Cost-Plus Markup

**Definition**: Charge the retail price (or a fixed percentage above cost), regardless of the client's size, usage, or value.

**Formula**: Sell Price = Cost + (Cost × Markup %)

**Example** (at Tier 3 discount, 40%):
- GHL Pro retail: $497
- Your cost: $298 (40% discount)
- Markup target: 67% (double your cost, rough rule of thumb)
- Sell price: $298 + ($298 × 0.67) = $298 + $200 = **$498/month** (retail price, essentially)
- **Monthly profit per client**: $498 − $298 = **$200/month**
- **Annual profit per client**: **$2,400/year**

**Pros**:
- Simple to explain and execute
- Clients recognize the retail price (no "why are you charging more?" questions)
- Predictable margins
- Easy to scale (same markup across all clients)

**Cons**:
- Low margin (only 40% above cost); limited profit cushion for support overruns
- Doesn't reflect client value—a 2-person agency and a 50-person agency pay the same
- Leaves money on the table for high-value clients
- Not defensible if a client finds GHL's partner page and realizes they could buy cheaper

**Best for**: Year 1 agencies, proof-of-concept, referral-driven sales (where pricing is less scrutinized).

---

### Model 2: Value-Based Pricing

**Definition**: Charge based on the client's perceived value from GHL, not your cost.

**Formula**: Sell Price = Client's Willingness to Pay (based on ROI, company size, feature usage)

**Segmentation example**:

| Segment | Profile | Features Used | ROI Benefit | Your Price | Cost | Profit/Month |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Solopreneur** | Coach, solo freelancer, <$50K revenue | CRM, email, SMS, forms | Saves 10 hrs/week | $299/month | $209 (Tier 2, 35%) | $90 |
| **Small Business** | 2–5 people, $100–300K revenue | CRM, automation, landing pages, SMS | Saves 20 hrs/week, faster intake | $499/month | $298 (Tier 3, 40%) | $201 |
| **Mid-Market** | 6–15 people, $500K–$2M revenue | Full Pro: CRM, automation, workflows, integrations, team seats | Improves cash flow by $5K/month | $749/month | $298 (Tier 3, 40%) | $451 |
| **Enterprise** | 15+ people, $2M+ revenue | Unlimited + white-label + dedicated support | Scales team to 100+ clients | $1,499/month | $598 (Tier 3, 40%) | $901 |

**Monthly revenue** (10 clients: 1 Solopreneur, 4 Small Business, 4 Mid-Market, 1 Enterprise):
- 1 × $299 = $299
- 4 × $499 = $1,996
- 4 × $749 = $2,996
- 1 × $1,499 = $1,499
- **Total revenue**: **$6,790/month** (vs. $4,970 on cost-plus model)
- **Total cost**: 1×$209 + 4×$298 + 4×$298 + 1×$598 = $2,489
- **Total profit**: $6,790 − $2,489 = **$4,301/month** (63% margin vs. 40% on cost-plus)

**Pricing conversation**:
1. Understand the client's business size, revenue, and pain points
2. Quantify the ROI: "GHL typically saves our clients 15–20 hours/week. At your team's blended rate, that's $X/month in labor value."
3. Price based on ROI: "We'll charge $Y/month, which captures 10–20% of your time savings. After 3 months, you've recouped our fee."

**Pros**:
- High margin (40–60% gross)
- Scales with client success (if client grows, they can afford higher prices)
- Aligns your incentives with theirs (you both win)
- Defensible pricing (you're delivering value, not reselling)

**Cons**:
- Requires sales skill (justifying price to clients)
- Need to segment and profile clients (time-intensive upfront)
- Clients may push back or shop around
- Support cost per client can still exceed profit if not careful

**Best for**: Year 2+ agencies with established processes, strong value prop, and sales team.

### Model 3: Tiered Pricing

**Definition**: Offer multiple plan tiers (Starter, Standard, Pro, Unlimited) under your own branding; clients choose the tier that fits their needs.

**Example tiered package**:

| Your Tier | Included GHL Plan | Features You Add | Your Price | Your Cost | Profit/Month |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | GHL Starter + onboarding | Email support, basic setup help | $179/month | $69 (Tier 1, 30%) | $110 |
| **Growth** | GHL Standard + onboarding + monthly check-in | Email + Slack support, 2 hours/month included | $349/month | $209 (Tier 2, 35%) | $140 |
| **Pro** | GHL Pro + onboarding + quarterly strategy | Email + Slack + phone support, quarterly business review | $649/month | $298 (Tier 3, 40%) | $351 |
| **Enterprise** | GHL Unlimited + dedicated support | Dedicated support person, monthly strategy, custom integrations | $1,299/month | $598 (Tier 3, 40%) | $701 |

**Adoption pattern** (typical agency experience):
- 40% of clients choose Starter (cost-conscious; small teams)
- 35% choose Growth (mid-sized; want support)
- 20% choose Pro (scaling; want advanced features)
- 5% choose Enterprise (large agencies; want white-label + dedicated support)

**Monthly revenue** (100 clients: 40 Starter, 35 Growth, 20 Pro, 5 Enterprise):
- 40 × $179 = $7,160
- 35 × $349 = $12,215
- 20 × $649 = $12,980
- 5 × $1,299 = $6,495
- **Total revenue**: **$38,850/month**

**Monthly cost** (at Tier 3 discount, 40%):
- 40 × $69 = $2,760
- 35 × $209 = $7,315
- 20 × $298 = $5,960
- 5 × $598 = $2,990
- **Total cost**: **$19,025/month**

**Monthly profit**: $38,850 − $19,025 = **$19,825/month** (51% gross margin)

**Pros**:
- Familiar to clients (similar to SaaS pricing models)
- Upsell path (start on Starter, move to Growth as they grow)
- Captures all market segments (solopreneurs to enterprises)
- Easier to sell ("Choose the tier that fits your budget")

**Cons**:
- Cannibalization risk (everyone buys the cheapest tier; you lose margin)
- Requires clear differentiation between tiers (what's included in support, features, etc.)
- More complex to manage (multiple tiers, different SLAs, support loads)

**Best for**: Agencies with diverse client bases, established support processes, and marketing/sales team.

## Part 3: Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) & Churn

### Calculating LTV

**Formula**: LTV = (Monthly profit per client) × (Average customer lifetime in months)

**Variables to estimate**:

| Variable | Estimate | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly profit per client | Sell price − cost | $200 (Pro at cost-plus) |
| Average customer lifetime | Months until churn | 30 months |
| **LTV** | — | $200 × 30 = **$6,000** |

### Churn Rates by Vertical

Churn varies by client type and your support quality:

| Vertical | Typical Monthly Churn | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Coaching** | 5–8% | High seasonality; Q1 churn spikes (New Year's resolution fade) |
| **Home Services** | 2–3% | Stable; long-term client relationships; low digital adoption pressure |
| **E-commerce** | 3–5% | Seasonal; churn spikes during slow seasons (summer, post-holiday) |
| **SaaS/Tech** | 1–3% | Low churn; integrated workflows; high switching cost |
| **Digital Marketing Agencies** | 4–6% | Variable; depends on client ROI performance; churn on poor results |
| **Average (all verticals)** | **2–5%** | 2% = good (60% annual retention); 5% = typical (41% annual retention) |

**Example LTV calculation** (Pro client at cost-plus, Tier 3 discount):

**Scenario A: Coaching (high churn, 5% monthly)**
- Monthly profit: $200/month
- Monthly churn: 5% (avg customer lifetime = 20 months before 95% are gone)
- LTV: $200 × 20 = **$4,000**

**Scenario B: Home Services (low churn, 2.5% monthly)**
- Monthly profit: $200/month
- Monthly churn: 2.5% (avg customer lifetime = 40 months)
- LTV: $200 × 40 = **$8,000**

**Scenario C: Tiered pricing, mixed clients (3% average monthly churn)**
- Avg monthly profit per client: $180 (mix of Starter, Growth, Pro)
- Avg customer lifetime: 33 months (1 ÷ 0.03)
- LTV: $180 × 33 = **$5,940**

### Payback Period (Customer Acquisition Cost ÷ LTV)

**Formula**: Payback = Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) ÷ Monthly Profit

**Examples**:

| CAC Source | CAC | Monthly Profit | Payback |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Referral (organic)** | $0 | $200 | Immediate |
| **Content (blog, YouTube)** | $200–$500 | $200 | 1–2.5 months |
| **Cold outreach (email, LinkedIn)** | $500–$1,000 | $200 | 2.5–5 months |
| **Paid ads (Google, Facebook)** | $1,000–$2,000 | $200 | 5–10 months |
| **Affiliate network** | $200–$500 | $200 | 1–2.5 months |

**Payback period target**: <6 months (industry standard for SaaS resale). If your payback is >12 months, your pricing or CAC is broken.

## Part 4: Support Costs & Profitability Models

### Support Cost Breakdown

**Three phases**:

1. **Onboarding (Month 0–3)**: 10–20 hours per client
   - Setup: Import contacts, configure workflows, test integrations
   - Training: 2–4 hours of video calls or screen-shares
   - Validation: Check client is using GHL before "done"
   - **Cost**: 10–20 hours × $40/hr (your blended rate) = $400–$800 per client

2. **Early Use (Month 4–12)**: 1–2 hours per month per client
   - Support tickets: Workflow issues, automation bugs, feature questions
   - Training calls: Advanced features, process optimization
   - **Cost**: 12 months × 1.5 hours × $40/hr = $720/year per client

3. **Maintenance (Month 13+)**: 1–2 hours per month per client
   - Ongoing support: Bug fixes, minor tweaks, escalations to GHL
   - Proactive outreach: Monthly check-in call or email
   - **Cost**: 12 months × 1 hour × $40/hr = $480/year per client

**Total annual support cost per client**:
- Year 1: $600 (onboarding amortized) + $720 (early use) = **$1,320/year**
- Year 2+: **$480/year** (maintenance only)

**At scale**:
- **10 clients, Year 1**: $1,320 × 10 = $13,200/year (you, solo, can handle at ~25 hours/week)
- **30 clients, Year 1**: $1,320 × 30 = $39,600/year (need part-time support, ~15 hours/week)
- **50 clients, Year 1**: $1,320 × 50 = $66,000/year (need full-time support specialist, ~40 hours/week)
- **50 clients, Year 2+**: $480 × 50 = $24,000/year (after onboarding, maintenance is light)
