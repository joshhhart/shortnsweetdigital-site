## Part 1: Understanding GHL for Accounting

### Why Accounting Firms Choose GoHighLevel

Accounting firms operate on **time and trust**. You bill by the hour (or by fixed fee), so reclaiming 10+ hours per week directly increases profitability. You also need **client confidence**—when clients see organized, timely communication and fast document processing, they renew and refer.

**Without GHL** (spreadsheet-based workflow):
- New client inquiry lands in email
- You manually create a spreadsheet row for the client
- You send an email asking for documents; no tracking, no reminder
- Client forgets or loses the email; you send 3+ follow-ups
- Documents trickle in over weeks; some arrive incomplete
- You manually chase invoices; payments arrive 45–60 days after sending
- **Time cost**: 5–7 hours/week of admin work; **Client satisfaction**: 6/10 (slow process, poor visibility)

**With GHL** (automated workflow):
- New client fills out intake form (auto-created contact)
- Automation triggers: SMS "Welcome! Please upload your docs here: [portal link]"
- Client logs into portal, uploads documents (all in one place)
- 48 hours later, automation sends reminder SMS (if docs incomplete)
- Documents arrive in portal, you review and approve
- Billing automation sends invoice + payment link on day 1
- Reminder SMS sent on day 10 if payment pending
- **Time cost**: 30 minutes/week of monitoring; **Client satisfaction**: 9/10 (fast, transparent, professional)

## Part 2: Setting Up Client Intake Forms

### Step 1: Create Your Intake Form in GHL

1. Log into **GoHighLevel**
2. Navigate to **Forms** (or **Contacts** → **Forms**)
3. Click **+ Create Form** (or **New Form**)
4. **Form name**: "Client Intake – [Your Firm Name]"
5. **Form type**: Select **Standalone Form** (or **Contact Form** depending on GHL version)
6. Click **Create**

### Step 2: Add Form Fields

Add fields in this order (required fields first, optional last):

#### Required Fields (Every Intake Needs These)

| Field Name | Field Type | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email | Email | Yes | GHL uses email as unique contact ID |
| First Name | Text | Yes | For personalization in automations |
| Last Name | Text | Yes | Complete contact name |
| Phone | Phone | Yes | For SMS reminders and direct contact |
| Business Name | Text | No | Client's company/entity name |

#### Conditional Fields (Add Based on Firm Type)

**For tax prep / bookkeeping firms:**
| Field Name | Field Type | Required | Sample Options |
|---|---|---|---|
| Service Type | Select (Single) | Yes | Tax Prep, Bookkeeping, Payroll, Consulting, Multi-service |
| Entity Type | Select (Single) | No | Sole Proprietor, S-Corp, C-Corp, LLC, Partnership |
| Year-End Date | Date | No | (Client selects their fiscal year-end) |
| Number of Employees | Select | No | 1, 2–5, 6–20, 21–50, 50+ |
| Estimated Annual Revenue | Select | No | <$100K, $100–250K, $250K–1M, $1M+ |

**For bookkeeping-only firms:**
| Field Name | Field Type | Required | Sample Options |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current Accounting Software | Select (Single) | No | QuickBooks Online, Xero, FreshBooks, None, Other |
| Monthly Transaction Volume | Select | No | <50, 50–200, 200–500, 500+ |

**For multi-service firms (add all applicable):**
- Payroll frequency (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly)
- 1099/W-2 split (percentage of contractors vs. employees)
- Current CPA or accountant (name, if switching)
- Timeline for onboarding (Immediate, Next month, Next quarter)

#### Add These For Every Firm

**Consent & Communication:**

| Field Name | Field Type | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preferred Contact Method | Select (Single) | Yes | Email, SMS, Phone, No Preference |
| Best Time to Contact | Select | No | Early morning, Midday, Late afternoon, Flexible |
| I agree to the [Privacy Policy](link) | Checkbox | Yes | Compliance for email/SMS consent |

#### Optional Nice-to-Haves

| Field Name | Field Type | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| How did you hear about us? | Select (Single) | No | Referral, Google, Facebook, LinkedIn, Other |
| Additional notes or questions | Long Text | No | Open-ended for client context |

### Step 3: Configure Form Settings

1. Click **Form Settings** (or **Configure**)
2. **Form title** (displayed at top): "Get Started – Client Intake"
3. **Form description**: "Tell us about your business. We'll review your information and contact you within 24 hours."
4. **Submit button text**: "Submit My Information"
5. **Post-submit message**:
   ```
   Thank you for submitting your information!

   We've received your details and will contact you within 24 hours.
   
   In the meantime, here's what to prepare:
   - Last 3 months of bank statements
   - Current year P&L or revenue summary
   - Any tax documents from your previous CPA
   
   We'll send you a secure portal link to upload documents.
   ```
6. **Color scheme** (optional): Match your firm's branding (logo, colors)
7. Click **Save**

### Step 4: Publish Form & Get Embed Code

1. Click **Publish** (or **Go Live**)
2. GHL generates a **form URL**: `https://[your-domain].gohighlevel.com/form/[form-id]`
3. Click **Get Embed Code** to copy the form for your website
4. Share the form link in email signature, website, LinkedIn, and ads:
   - Website: "Get Started" button
   - Email signature: Linked in call-to-action
   - Facebook/Google ads: Direct link to form

**Example email signature**:
```
Ready to streamline your accounting? 
Get started → [https://your-domain.gohighlevel.com/form/client-intake]
```

## Part 3: Automating Document Collection

### Step 1: Create the Document Request Automation

1. In **GoHighLevel**, navigate to **Automations** → **Create New Workflow**
2. **Workflow name**: "Client Onboarding – Document Collection"
3. **Start Trigger**: Select **Form Submitted** → Choose your intake form
4. Click **Create**

### Step 2: Add Immediate Welcome SMS

1. Click **+ Add Action**
2. Select **Send SMS**
3. **To**: {{phoneNumber}}
4. **From**: Your GHL business phone number
5. **Message**:
   ```
   Hi {{firstName}}, welcome to [Your Firm Name]! 🎉

   We've received your information. Next step: upload your documents to your secure portal.

   👉 [PORTAL_LINK]

   Questions? Reply to this message or call [your phone].
   ```
6. Click **Save**

**Note**: Replace `[PORTAL_LINK]` with the GHL client portal link (explained in Part 4). For now, leave it as a placeholder.

### Step 3: Create a Task for Your Team

1. Click **+ Add Action**
2. Select **Create Task** (or **Assign Task**)
3. **Task name**: "Review intake form: {{firstName}} {{lastName}}"
4. **Description**:
   ```
   Service type: {{serviceType}}
   Entity: {{entityType}}
   Est. revenue: {{estimatedAnnualRevenue}}
   Timeline: {{onboardingTimeline}}
   Notes: {{additionalNotes}}
   ```
5. **Assigned to**: Select the team member responsible for onboarding
6. **Due date**: Today
7. Click **Save**

### Step 4: Add a 48-Hour Document Reminder

1. Click **+ Add Action**
2. Select **Delay** → **2 days** (48 hours)
3. Click **Save**

### Step 5: Add Conditional Check (Documents Uploaded?)

1. Click **+ Add Action**
2. Select **Conditional Branch** (or **Decision**)
3. **Condition type**: "Contact property"
4. **Condition**: "If documents uploaded to portal >= 1"
   - (This checks if the contact has uploaded any files; GHL tracks this automatically)
5. Click **Save**

### Step 6: If Documents Uploaded (True Branch)

1. In the **If True** branch, click **+ Add Action**
2. Select **Send SMS**
3. **Message**:
   ```
   Great! {{firstName}}, we received your documents. 

   We're reviewing them now and will contact you within 1 business day with next steps.

   Thank you for being organized!
   ```
4. Click **Save**
5. Click **+ Add Action**
6. Select **Add Tag** → "docs-collected"
7. Click **Save**

### Step 7: If Documents Not Uploaded (False Branch)

1. In the **If False** branch, click **+ Add Action**
2. Select **Send SMS**
3. **Message**:
   ```
   {{firstName}}, quick reminder: we're still waiting for your documents. 

   Upload here: [PORTAL_LINK]

   Need help? Reply or call [your phone] for instructions.
   ```
4. Click **Save**
5. Click **+ Add Action**
6. Select **Add Tag** → "awaiting-docs"
7. Click **Save**

### Step 8: Publish the Automation

1. Click **Publish** (top right)
2. Confirm: "Automation is now active"
3. **Status**: Active (green indicator)

**Result**: Every new client who submits the form automatically receives:
- Welcome SMS within seconds
- Task created for your team to review
- 48-hour reminder SMS
- Follow-up based on document upload status

### Step 1: Enable & Configure the Client Portal

The **GHL client portal** is a password-protected space where clients upload documents, view invoices, and access firm resources.

1. In **GoHighLevel**, navigate to **Settings** → **Portal** (or **Contacts** → **Portal**)
2. Click **Enable Portal**
3. **Portal name**: "[Your Firm Name] Client Hub" or "Secure Client Portal"
4. **Branding**:
   - Upload your firm logo (ideal size: 200×100 px)
   - Set primary color (match your brand)
   - Add a custom domain (optional): `clients.yourfirm.com` (requires domain setup)
5. Click **Save**

### Step 2: Create Portal Sections (Optional but Recommended)

Organize client resources into sections:

1. Click **+ Add Portal Section**
2. **Section name**: "Documents & Uploads"
3. **Description**: "Upload tax docs, receipts, and business records here"
4. Click **Save**
5. Repeat for additional sections:
   - "Invoices & Payments"
   - "Resources & Guides"
   - "Contact & Support"

### Step 3: Generate Client Portal Link

1. In **GHL**, navigate to **Contacts** → Select a contact
2. Scroll down to **Portal Access** (or **Client Portal**)
3. Click **Generate Portal Link** (or **Enable Portal Access**)
4. GHL generates a unique URL: `https://portal.gohighlevel.com/[contact-id]`
5. This link is contact-specific; each client gets their own login
6. Copy this link and add it to your SMS templates and intake automation (replace `[PORTAL_LINK]`)

**To generate links in bulk** (for existing clients):
1. Navigate to **Contacts** → **All Contacts**
2. Click **Bulk Actions** → **Generate Portal Links**
3. Select all or specific contacts
4. GHL generates portal access for all selected contacts
5. Download the list (shows contact email + unique portal link)
6. Send the links to clients via email or SMS

## Part 5: Automating Billing & Invoicing

### Step 1: Create the Billing Automation

1. Navigate to **Automations** → **Create New Workflow**
2. **Workflow name**: "Monthly Billing – Invoice & Reminder"
3. **Start Trigger**: Select **Scheduled Date** (or **Recurring Date**)
4. **Date**: Month-end (or your preferred billing date, e.g., the 1st of next month)
5. **Frequency**: Monthly
6. Click **Create**

### Step 2: Create Invoice & Send via SMS

1. Click **+ Add Action**
2. Select **Create Invoice** (or **Send Invoice**)
   - (Note: If GHL does not have native invoicing, use **Send Email** with an invoice attachment or Zapier bridge to QuickBooks/Xero)
3. **Invoice details**:
   - **Amount**: $[amount] (enter manually or link to custom field if using variable billing)
   - **Description**: "Professional accounting services – [Month]"
   - **Due date**: 15 days from invoice date
   - **Payment method**: Link to GHL's payment processor or Stripe
4. Click **Save**
5. Click **+ Add Action**
6. Select **Send SMS** (after invoice created)
7. **Message**:
   ```
   Hi {{firstName}}, your monthly bill for [Month] is ready.

   Amount: $[amount]
   Due: [due date]

   Pay here: [payment link]

   Questions? Reply or call [phone].
   ```
8. Click **Save**

### Step 3: Add Payment Reminder (10 Days Later)

1. Click **+ Add Action**
2. Select **Delay** → **10 days**
3. Click **Save**
4. Click **+ Add Action**
5. Select **Conditional Branch**
6. **Condition**: "If payment status = unpaid" (or "If invoice balance > 0")
7. Click **Save**

### Step 4: Send Reminder SMS (If Unpaid)

1. In the **If True** (unpaid) branch, click **+ Add Action**
2. Select **Send SMS**
3. **Message**:
   ```
   {{firstName}}, friendly reminder: your invoice from [date] is due in 5 days.

   Current balance: $[amount]

   Pay now: [payment link]

   Need an extension? Call us at [phone].
   ```
4. Click **Save**

### Step 5: Create a Task for Overdue Invoices (20 Days Unpaid)

1. Click **+ Add Action**
2. Select **Delay** → **20 days** (from original invoice date)
3. Click **Save**
4. Click **+ Add Action**
5. Select **Create Task**
6. **Task name**: "Follow up: {{firstName}} – Invoice overdue 20 days"
7. **Description**: "Amount: $[amount]. Contact client to resolve payment."
8. **Assigned to**: Your office manager or collections person
9. **Due date**: Today
10. Click **Save**

### Step 6: Publish the Billing Automation

1. Click **Publish**
2. Confirm active status

**Result**: Invoices are sent automatically on your chosen date, reminders go out 10 days later if unpaid, and your team is alerted to overdue accounts at 20 days.

### Automation: Weekly Status Update SMS

**Goal**: Send clients a brief progress update every Friday, reducing "where are we?" phone calls.

#### Setup Steps

1. Navigate to **Automations** → **Create New Workflow**
2. **Workflow name**: "Weekly Status Update – SMS"
3. **Start Trigger**: **Scheduled Date** → Friday, 10:00 AM
4. **Frequency**: Weekly
5. Click **Create**

#### Add Status Update SMS

1. Click **+ Add Action**
2. Select **Send SMS**
3. **Message** (customize for service type):

   ```
   Hi {{firstName}}, here's your weekly update on your {{serviceType}} account:

   ✓ Week of [date]: Processed {{numberOfTransactions}} transactions
   ✓ Current status: On track for [milestone]
   ✓ Next: [Next step, e.g., "Review of Q3 expenses"]

   Questions? Reply YES or call [phone].
   ```

4. Click **Save**

#### Add Conditional: If Client Replies YES

1. Click **+ Add Action**
2. Select **Conditional Branch** → "If SMS reply contains YES or thumbs up"
3. Click **Save**

#### If Client Wants to Talk

1. In the **If True** branch, click **+ Add Action**
2. Select **Create Task**
3. **Task name**: "Call {{firstName}} – requested follow-up"
4. **Assigned to**: Primary contact person
5. **Due date**: Today
6. Click **Save**
7. Click **+ Add Action** (still in True branch)
8. Select **Send SMS**
9. **Message**: "Perfect! Someone will call you by end of day. Thank you!"
10. Click **Save**

#### If No Reply (False Branch)

1. In the **If False** branch, click **+ Add Action**
2. Select **Add Tag** → "status-acknowledged"
3. Click **Save**

#### Publish

1. Click **Publish**

**Result**: Every Friday at 10 AM, clients receive a progress update. If they reply "YES", your team is immediately notified to call. Otherwise, they're tagged as acknowledged.

## Part 7: Tax Deadline Prep Automation

### Automation: 60/30/14 Day Tax Deadline Reminders

**Goal**: Send clients reminders at 60, 30, and 14 days before their tax deadline, with specific action items.

#### Setup

1. Create a custom field in GHL Contacts:
   - **Field name**: "Tax Deadline"
   - **Field type**: "Date"
   - Add this date to intake form (ask clients their fiscal year-end)

2. Create automation: **Scheduled Date** → Select the tax deadline date

3. **First reminder** (60 days before): Create three sequential automations (60-day, 30-day, 14-day), or use a single automation with delays:

   **60-Day Reminder SMS**:
   ```
   {{firstName}}, tax season is approaching!

   Your deadline: {{taxDeadline}}

   📋 To-do list:
   • Gather 2025 bank statements
   • Collect receipts (mileage, meals, supplies)
   • Provide Q4 profit/loss statement
   • Report any business structure changes

   Upload docs here: [PORTAL_LINK]

   Questions? Call [phone].
   ```

   **30-Day Reminder SMS**:
   ```
   {{firstName}}, 30 days until your tax deadline!

   Still need:
   • [Specific missing docs from previous interaction]

   Please upload ASAP to avoid delays.

   [PORTAL_LINK]
   ```

   **14-Day Reminder SMS**:
   ```
   {{firstName}}, 2 weeks until your deadline ({{taxDeadline}}).

   Final call: If you haven't uploaded documents, do it TODAY.

   We'll need 3–5 business days to prepare your return.

   [PORTAL_LINK] | Call [phone] if urgent.
   ```

4. Add delays between messages:
   - Delay 30 days after 60-day reminder
   - Delay 16 days after 30-day reminder
   - Publish and activate

**Result**: Clients receive automated reminders at critical milestones, reducing last-minute rushes and ensuring timely document submission.

## Part 8: Team Collaboration & Handoff Workflows

### Automation: Assign Contacts to Team Members

**Goal**: Route new clients to the right team member based on service type (bookkeeper, tax specialist, payroll specialist).

#### Setup

1. Navigate to **Automations** → **Create New Workflow**
2. **Workflow name**: "Client Routing – Assign to Specialist"
3. **Start Trigger**: **Form Submitted** → Intake form
4. Click **Create**

#### Add Conditional Routing

1. Click **+ Add Action**
2. Select **Conditional Branch**
3. **Condition**: "If Service Type = Bookkeeping"
4. Click **Save**

#### If Bookkeeping

1. In **If True** branch, click **+ Add Action**
2. Select **Assign Contact to User** (or **Route to Team Member**)
3. **User**: Select your bookkeeper
4. Click **Save**
5. Click **+ Add Action**
6. Select **Send Email** (to bookkeeper)
7. **Subject**: "New bookkeeping client: {{firstName}} {{lastName}}"
8. **Body**:
   ```
   A new bookkeeping client has been assigned to you.

   Name: {{firstName}} {{lastName}}
   Email: {{email}}
   Phone: {{phone}}
   Company: {{businessName}}
   Monthly transactions: {{monthlyTransactionVolume}}

   Next step: Review intake form in task panel and reach out to confirm engagement.
   ```
9. Click **Save**

#### Else If Tax Prep

1. Click **+ Add Condition** (in original branch)
2. **Condition**: "Else if Service Type = Tax Prep"
3. Click **Save**
4. In this **If True** branch:
   - Assign contact to your tax specialist
   - Send email to tax specialist with similar details

#### Else (Multi-Service or Other)

1. Click **+ Add Condition**
2. **Condition**: "Else"
3. Click **Save**
4. In **If False** branch:
   - Assign to your managing partner or lead accountant for routing decision

#### Publish

1. Click **Publish**

**Result**: Incoming clients are automatically routed to the right specialist based on their service needs. No manual assignment necessary.
