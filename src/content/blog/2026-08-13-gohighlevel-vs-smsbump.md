### 3. SMS Deliverability: Open Rates and Compliance

Both platforms use carrier-grade SMS infrastructure (Twilio, Bandwidth, Plivo). Deliverability depends more on list quality and compliance than platform choice.

| Metric | GoHighLevel | SMSBump | Winner |
|--------|-------------|---------|--------|
| **SMS carrier** | Twilio + Bandwidth | Twilio + others | Tie |
| **Average open rate** | 28–35% (industry standard) | 30–38% (SMS-optimized) | SMSBump (slightly higher) |
| **Bounce rate** | 2–5% | 1–3% | SMSBump (slightly better) |
| **List warm-up support** | Basic (documentated) | Advanced (built-in warm-up workflow) | SMSBump |
| **Compliance (TCPA/GDPR)** | Documented; your responsibility | Documented; your responsibility | Tie |
| **Compliance penalties** | Very rare; user error | Very rare; user error | Tie |

**Verdict**: SMSBump has a slight edge on SMS deliverability because SMS is its core focus. However, both platforms achieve 98%+ deliverability on clean lists. The difference matters only if you're sending to low-quality lists; with proper list hygiene, both are equivalent.

**Critical compliance note**: Both platforms place compliance responsibility on you (the user). Sending unsolicited SMS violates TCPA (US) and GDPR (EU). Always:
- Get explicit SMS consent from recipients (checkbox on signup form, double opt-in)
- Provide easy unsubscribe (STOP reply)
- Keep consent records (proof that user opted in)
- Avoid sending to numbers that texted STOP

Violating TCPA can result in $500–$1,500 per message fines. Both platforms enforce compliance, but enforcement is reactive (they'll suspend accounts after complaints, not prevent violations upfront).

**Solution**: Audit your 2-year roadmap upfront. If email or CRM is even a possibility, choose GoHighLevel. Cost is similar; flexibility is vastly higher.
