## Part 1: White-Label Fundamentals & Economics

### What is GHL White-Label?

**Definition**: You purchase GHL accounts at a wholesale rate (typically $40–$150/month depending on tier), rebrand them with your company name and logo, and resell to clients at retail price ($99–$299/month). You keep the margin; GHL handles platform maintenance.

### White-Label vs. Referral vs. Agency Partner

| Model | Margin | Recurring | Branding | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **White-Label** | 40–60% | ✅ Yes | Your logo, color, domain | You own it all |
| **Referral** | 20–30% | ❌ No (one-time) | GHL branding | GHL owns support |
| **Agency Partner** | 10–20% | ✅ Yes (small) | GHL + your name | Shared |
| **Reseller (Large)** | 30–40% | ✅ Yes | Your branding | You own it |

**For recurring revenue**, white-label is the clear winner.

### Economics at Scale

**Scenario: 50 White-Label Clients at $150/Month**

| Item | Cost/Month | Year 1 |
|---|---|---|
| **Revenue** (50 clients × $150) | $7,500 | $90,000 |
| **GHL Wholesale** (50 × $70 avg) | $3,500 | $42,000 |
| **Support Time** (3 hrs/mo per 10 clients) | $450 | $5,400 |
| **Marketing & Platform Overhead** | $300 | $3,600 |
| **Net Margin (Before Taxes)** | **$3,250** | **$38,000** |

**Profitability threshold**: 20–30 clients. Below that, support overhead eats margin. Above 80 clients, you need to automate onboarding or hire support staff.

## Part 3: Snapshot 2 — Home Services (Plumbing)

**Client Profile**: 3-person plumbing company, Seattle. $400K/year revenue, 15–20 jobs/month.

**Problem**: Appointment reminders were manual (owner called customers day-before). 35% no-show rate cost $8K+/month in wasted technician time. Invoicing was via QuickBooks; no payment reminders. Customer communication was siloed (owner used personal phone, texts were untracked).

**Solution**: White-label GHL with:
- Appointment booking page (clients book directly; auto-syncs to GHL)
- SMS reminders (48 hours + 4 hours before appointment)
- Payment processing (Stripe → GHL; invoice PDF sent automatically)
- Team calendar view (all technicians see scheduled jobs in GHL)

### Results (3 Months)

| Metric | Before | After | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **No-Show Rate** | 35% | 12% | ✅ 66% reduction |
| **Jobs Completed/Month** | 18 | 24 | ✅ 33% increase |
| **Monthly Revenue** | $400K/yr ÷ 12 = $33K | $44K | ✅ 33% increase |
| **Invoice Payment Time** | 30 days avg | 5 days avg | ✅ 83% faster |
| **Time on Admin** | 12 hrs/week | 3 hrs/week | ✅ 75% reduction |
| **Customer Satisfaction** | 3.8/5 | 4.6/5 | ✅ 21% improvement |

**Revenue impact**: 33% revenue increase = +$132K annually. Minus white-label cost ($70/month × 12 = $840) = **$131K net new annual revenue**.

**Key win**: No-show reduction is the profit lever for appointment-based businesses. Each appointment no-show costs $80–$150 (wasted technician time + truck cost). 66% reduction = ~20 fewer no-shows/month × $100 = $24K saved annually.

**Lessons**:
1. SMS reminders reduce no-shows 25–35% for any appointment business (salons, medical, contractors)
2. Payment processing integration (Stripe → invoice → reminder) accelerates cash flow; ROI is immediate
3. For small teams (2–5 people), calendar visibility saves coordination overhead

## Part 9: Onboarding Playbook (Replicate These Steps)

### Week 1: Discovery & Planning

**Client Call (30 min)**
1. Understand their top 3 business problems
2. Map to 1 white-label automation you can deliver in 30 days
3. Agree on success metric (e.g., "reduce appointment no-shows from 30% to 15%")
4. Schedule 3 follow-up calls (week 2, week 3, week 4)

**Your Prep (60 min)**
1. Create client sandbox account in white-label GHL
2. Pre-populate with sample data (10 sample contacts, 2 sample automations)
3. Record 3 video walkthroughs (5 min each):
   - How to add a contact
   - How to create an automation
   - How to view reports
4. Prepare vertical-specific template (3 pre-built automations ready to activate)

### Week 2: Setup & Integration

**Client Call (30 min)**
1. Walkthrough sandbox account; let them add 2–3 real contacts
2. Show template automations; help them customize language
3. Set up integrations (Zapier, Stripe, Shopify, etc. as needed)
4. Test: Send test email/SMS; confirm it works

**Your Prep (45 min)**
1. Set up integrations (connect Zapier, payment processor, etc.)
2. Create automation triggers (lead form → Zapier → GHL)
3. Verify data flows end-to-end

### Week 3: Training & Go-Live

**Client Call (30 min)**
1. Client activates first automation in production
2. Test with real data (real customer email/SMS)
3. Review analytics dashboard
4. Celebrate the first win (email sent, SMS delivered, contact added)

**Your Prep (30 min)**
1. Monitor dashboard for errors in first 48 hours
2. Fix any integration hiccups
3. Send follow-up email with "next steps" (second automation, advanced features)

### Week 4: Optimization & Expansion

**Client Call (30 min)**
1. Review results from Week 3 automation
   - Emails sent: X, opened: Y, clicked: Z
   - SMS sent: X, delivered: Y
   - Contacts added: X, engaged: Y
2. Identify second automation to implement (upsell, retention, SMS, etc.)
3. Schedule monthly check-in (same time, first Monday of month)

**Your Prep (45 min)**
1. Analyze data; suggest optimizations (subject line, send time, SMS copy)
2. Document lessons learned for this client (vertical-specific gotchas, customizations)
3. Update template based on what worked

### Success Metrics to Track

By end of week 4, client should see:
- ✅ Minimum 50 contacts in GHL
- ✅ 1 active automation running (emails/SMS sending)
- ✅ 1 integration working (Zapier, Stripe, Shopify, etc.)
- ✅ Dashboard visible (client can see metrics)
- ✅ Client sent at least 2 emails or SMS manually (proves they understand platform)

If any of the above missing: diagnosis is likely weak onboarding. Go back and re-do week 1–2.

### Why 60% Margin Matters

- **Support costs**: At $99/month, 2% churn = 1 client lost per 50. Replacing 50 clients costs $500–$1K in onboarding time. Better to over-deliver and earn <3% churn.
- **Scaling infrastructure**: Beyond 100 clients, you need help (contractor for support, automation tools for onboarding). Budget 15–20% of margin for this.
- **Competitive pressure**: At $99, you compete on price. At $180, you compete on value (
