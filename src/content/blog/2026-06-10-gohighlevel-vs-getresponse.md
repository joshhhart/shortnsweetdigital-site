# GoHighLevel vs GetResponse: Which Platform Wins for Agencies in 2026?

You're building an agency. You need a platform. Your options: GoHighLevel or GetResponse.

Both are all-in-one marketing automation platforms. Both offer CRM, email, SMS, funnels, and automation. Both have landing page builders. Both claim to replace multiple tools.

But they're not the same. And for agencies specifically, one is clearly better suited.

In 2026, GetResponse dominates the email marketing + landing page space (stronger email deliverability, better drag-and-drop builder). GoHighLevel dominates the agency CRM space (stronger SMS, better lead tracking, white-label SaaS Mode).

This guide compares the two platform-by-platform. We'll cover pricing, features, integrations, ease of use, and the one deciding factor that matters most for your agency.

> **Key Takeaways**
> - GoHighLevel is purpose-built for agencies (white-label, recurring commission, SMS focus); GetResponse is built for email marketing (stronger deliverability, better builder)
> - Pricing: GoHighLevel $99–$497/month per agency; GetResponse $15–$299/month (more flexible for scaling)
> - GetResponse has superior email deliverability (96%+ average inboxing rate vs. GoHighLevel's 91%); GoHighLevel has superior SMS delivery (99.2% vs. GetResponse's 95%)
> - Feature comparison (8 categories): GetResponse leads on email/landing pages (8/10 vs. 7/10); GoHighLevel leads on SMS/CRM/workflows (9/10 vs. 6/10)
> - For solo freelancers: GetResponse wins (lower cost, better email); for agencies with SMS focus: GoHighLevel wins (white-label, team management, lead scoring)
> - For hybrids (email + SMS + funnels): GetResponse at $299/mo + Twilio SMS ($0.005/msg) often costs less than GoHighLevel but requires more integration work

## How to Choose: Decision Matrix

Use this matrix to decide which platform is right for your agency:

### Choose GoHighLevel if...

- ✅ You want SMS as a core offering (included in plan, excellent delivery)
- ✅ You want to build a white-label SaaS Mode business (passive recurring revenue)
- ✅ You manage 10+ clients (cheaper at scale)
- ✅ You need advanced CRM (team roles, lead scoring, activity tracking)
- ✅ You want to use workflows as a service (sell automation to clients)
- ✅ You prioritize team collaboration (multiple users managing one account)

### Choose GetResponse if...

- ✅ You're a solo freelancer or very small agency (lower cost to start)
- ✅ Email marketing is your primary offering (superior deliverability and templates)
- ✅ You want the easiest platform to learn (GetResponse's UI is more intuitive)
- ✅ You focus on landing pages and funnels (better funnel builder)
- ✅ You don't need SMS (avoid Twilio integration complexity)
- ✅ You prefer flexible, usage-based pricing (pay as you grow)

### The Hybrid Approach: GetResponse + Twilio + Zapier

If you want GetResponse's email strength + SMS capabilities without going all-in on GoHighLevel:

1. Use GetResponse for email + landing pages ($99–$299/month)
2. Add Twilio for SMS ($0.01 per message; ~$500/month for 50K SMS)
3. Connect via Zapier (workflows between the two, ~$20/month)
4. Total cost: ~$620/month for 30 clients

**Pros**: Lower cost than GoHighLevel; best email deliverability; GetResponse's funnel templates

**Cons**: Integration complexity; SMS is a separate cost (scales with volume); no white-label SaaS Mode

**Best for**: Agencies that prioritize email quality over SMS depth; willing to integrate multiple tools

## FAQ

### Q: Can I use both GoHighLevel and GetResponse at the same time?

**A**: Yes, but it's usually inefficient. Use case: One team uses GetResponse for email (shared clients), another team uses GoHighLevel for CRM (separate clients). Keep them separate to avoid contact sync chaos.

---

### Q: Does GetResponse have a white-label option comparable to GoHighLevel SaaS Mode?

**A**: No. GetResponse offers branding customization (logo, colors, custom domain), but not full white-label SaaS Mode with recurring commissions. For white-label business models, GoHighLevel is superior.

---

### Q: Is GoHighLevel's email deliverability worse than GetResponse?

**A**: Slightly worse (91% vs. 96%). But for SMB-focused campaigns, the difference is rarely the deciding factor. If email is your primary service, GetResponse's 5% advantage compounds. If email is one of 5 services you offer, the difference is negligible.

---

### Q: Can I use GoHighLevel for email only (without SMS)?

**A**: Yes. SMS is just a feature; you don't have to use it. But you're paying for it in the plan, so you might as well leverage it. For email-only agencies, GetResponse makes more sense (you don't pay for unused SMS).

---

### Q: Which platform is better for e-commerce?

**A**: GetResponse has stronger e-commerce features (product recommendation automation, abandoned cart templates, post-purchase sequences). For e-commerce agencies, GetResponse is the better choice.

GoHighLevel is better for service-based businesses (agencies, coaches, consultants, local services).

---

### Q: Does GetResponse have SMS?

**A**: GetResponse added SMS in 2024, but it's not included in all plans. SMS costs $0.01–0.05 per message (on top of your plan). For high-volume SMS agencies, this makes GetResponse more expensive than GoHighLevel.

---

### Q: Which has better compliance (GDPR, CAN-SPAM)?

**A**: Both are compliant. GetResponse is slightly more aggressive with compliance reminders. GoHighLevel enforces TCPA rules automatically. For regulatory-sensitive industries, either platform works.

---

### Q: Can I try both before deciding?

**A**: Yes. Both offer free trials:
- **GoHighLevel**: 14-day free trial (all features)

Recommendation: Trial both for 2 weeks. Set up one client in each. See which feels more natural for your workflow.
