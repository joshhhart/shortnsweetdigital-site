## White-Label SaaS Mode: How Recurring Revenue Works

This is the most important feature to understand if you're considering Unlimited.

### The Commission Structure (Tiered)

GoHighLevel pays you a recurring commission on every client subscription you sell in SaaS Mode:

| Client Volume | Commission | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1–10 white-label clients | 40% | Sell 5 clients at $299/month each = $1,495/month revenue. GoHighLevel takes $897, you keep $598. |
| 11–50 white-label clients | 45% | Sell 25 clients at $299/month each = $7,475/month revenue. You keep 45% = $3,364/month. |
| 51+ white-label clients | 50% | Sell 100 clients at $299/month each = $29,900/month revenue. You keep 50% = $14,950/month. |

The commission scales up—the more clients you sell, the higher your cut.

### How It Works in Practice

**Step 1: Client signs up**

Your prospect visits your white-label portal (app.youragency.com). They see your logo, your branding, your pricing. They don't see "GoHighLevel" anywhere.

**Step 2: Client pays**

Client subscribes to your "Pro" plan ($299/month). You invoice them directly (or GoHighLevel processes the payment on your behalf). Payment hits your bank account.

**Step 3: You get the commission**

GoHighLevel deducts their cost from your client's payment and sends you the commission. You keep 40–50% recurring (month after month) as long as the client is active.

**Example**:
- Client pays you $299/month
- You keep 45% (your tier) = $134.55
- GoHighLevel keeps 55% = $164.45
- Client stays for 24 months: $134.55 × 24 = $3,229.20 passive revenue from one client

Scale this to 50 clients: $3,229 × 50 = $161,450/year in commission revenue.

## FAQ: GoHighLevel Pricing & Plans

### Q: Can I start on Starter and upgrade later without losing data?

**A**: Yes. All your contacts, campaigns, workflows, and automations stay intact when you upgrade. You can switch from Starter → Pro → Unlimited anytime. No data loss, no migration needed.

### Q: What happens to my SMS if I downgrade from Unlimited to Pro?

**A**: Your SMS limit drops from unlimited to 5,000/month. If you're using more, you'll hit the limit and won't be able to send additional SMS until next month. You won't lose your SMS history; just your ability to send beyond 5,000.

**Recommendation**: Check your monthly SMS usage before downgrading.

---

### Q: Is there a setup fee?

**A**: No. GoHighLevel charges only the monthly plan fee. No setup, no onboarding fee, no annual fee (unless you opt for annual billing).

---

### Q: Can I use the same GoHighLevel account for multiple agencies?

**A**: No. Each plan (Starter, Pro, Unlimited) is for one primary account. If you own multiple agencies, you'd need multiple GoHighLevel subscriptions.

Exception: Unlimited plan white-label mode lets you manage multiple client accounts under one primary account. But each of those client accounts is separate.

---

### Q: Do I have to pay for unused SMS if I'm on a monthly plan?

**A**: Yes. You pay the monthly plan fee upfront. If you're on Pro ($199/month with 5,000 SMS), you pay $199 regardless of whether you send 10 or 5,000 SMS.

**To minimize waste**: Start on Starter ($99, 500 SMS). Upgrade to Pro when you consistently hit 500 SMS/month.

---

### Q: If I sell white-label, do I set the client's price, or does GoHighLevel?

**A**: You set the price. You're reselling it as your platform under your brand. You can charge $99/month, $299/month, or $999/month; GoHighLevel takes 40–50% of whatever you charge (depending on your tier), and you keep the rest.

**A**: The commission structure is per-month, charged monthly. If your client pays annually (12 months upfront), you still receive 40–50% of that annual payment upfront, then month-to-month commission going forward.

Example:
- Client pays $349/month (or $4,188/year upfront)

**A**: Yes. GoHighLevel lets you set trial periods (7, 14, or 30 days). During the trial, the client doesn't pay; you don't pay commission. When the trial ends, they're charged (or the subscription cancels).

### Q: Is there a contract or minimum commitment?

**A**: No. Month-to-month billing; cancel anytime. No lock-in.
