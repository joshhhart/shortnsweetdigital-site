## Where Pro Hits Its Ceiling (And Unlimited Shines)

### Problem 1: User Limits on Pro

Pro includes **2 team users**. If you hire a 3rd person (which happens around 500+ contacts), you'll pay an extra $50-75/month per user.

**Scenario**: You're a 3-person agency on Pro plan.

**Cost breakdown**:
- Pro base: $297/mo
- User 3: +$50/mo
- **Effective cost: $347/mo**

**With Unlimited**:
- Unlimited base: $497/mo (includes 5 users)
- User 3: +$0
- User 4: +$0
- User 5: +$0
- **Effective cost: $497/mo (for 5 users)**

At 3+ people, **Unlimited is cheaper or equal cost** compared to Pro + extra user fees.

### Problem 2: White-Label Limitations on Pro

Pro gives you a **basic white-label portal**—clients see your branding on the login page. But the actual CRM interface shows GoHighLevel branding.

With Unlimited, you get a **full white-label experience**. Clients see your domain (youragency.gohighlevel.com), your logo everywhere, your colors—it looks like your product, not GoHighLevel's.

**Why this matters**: If you're reselling GoHighLevel to clients or positioning yourself as a platform provider, Pro's partial white-label feels incomplete. Clients will notice "Powered by GoHighLevel" branding, reducing perceived value.

**Example**: A client paying you $500/mo for a white-label CRM sees "GoHighLevel" in the interface. They wonder: "Why am I paying you $500 when I could use GoHighLevel directly for $297?" Unlimited eliminates this issue.

### Problem 3: Workflow Limitations

Pro caps automations at **50 workflows**. If you build automations for each client, you'll hit this limit with 10-15 clients.

Unlimited supports **500+ workflows**, letting you build separate automation suites for dozens of clients without hitting limits.

**Impact**: On Pro, you'll need to choose between building rich automations or managing multiple clients. On Unlimited, you can do both.

### Problem 4: API Rate Limits

Pro limits API requests to **600/hour**. Zapier and Make.com integrations count toward this limit.

If you're syncing leads across 10 clients + running Zapier automations, you might hit this ceiling. Unlimited raises the limit to **2,000/hour**, eliminating bottlenecks.

## FAQ

### Q: Can I switch from Pro to Unlimited mid-month?

**A**: Yes. GoHighLevel prorates. If you upgrade from Pro to Unlimited on the 15th of the month, you'll pay the difference for the remaining 15 days. Next month, you'll be billed the full Unlimited amount.

### Q: What happens if I downgrade from Unlimited back to Pro?

**A**: You can downgrade anytime. However:
- If you have 3+ team users, you'll lose access for users 3+ (they'll be suspended)
- If you have 51+ workflows, you'll lose access to workflows 51+ (they'll be paused)
- Your white-label subdomain reverts to basic GoHighLevel branding

**Recommendation**: Only downgrade if you're truly scaling down (firing people, losing clients). It's a painful process.

### Q: Is there a long-term contract or lock-in period?

**A**: No. GoHighLevel is month-to-month. Cancel anytime (though they'll ask why in an exit survey).

### Q: Can I use Pro if I have more than 2 team members by sharing one login?

**A**: Technically yes, but not recommended. Multiple people sharing one login causes:
- No audit trail (who made which change?)
- Password sharing (security risk)
- Accidental overwrites (two people editing the same workflow simultaneously)

**Recommendation**: Buy the extra user seat ($50-75/mo). It's cheaper than the operational chaos of shared logins.

### Q: Does Unlimited include more contacts than Pro?

**A**: No. Both Pro and Unlimited include unlimited contacts. The limit isn't number of contacts—it's number of team users, workflows, API requests, and custom fields.

### Q: Should I start with Pro or jump straight to Unlimited?

**A**: **Start with Pro if**:
- You're solo or a 2-person team
- You're testing GoHighLevel before full commitment
- You don't need to resell to clients

**Jump to Unlimited if**:
- You're already a 3+ person team
- You're planning to resell GoHighLevel
- You anticipate needing 500+ contacts within 6 months

### Q: What if I outgrow Unlimited?

**A**: GoHighLevel doesn't have a tier above Unlimited. Instead, they offer **enterprise customization** ($5,000+/mo) for very large agencies (100+ team members, 10,000+ contacts).

For 95% of agencies, Unlimited is the final tier you'll need.

### Q: Is the $297 Pro price locked in, or does it increase?

**A**: GoHighLevel occasionally raises prices for new signups but honors existing customers' prices. If you're on Pro at $297/mo, you'll likely keep that price (unless you downgrade and re-upgrade).

**Recommendation**: Don't downgrade if you plan to upgrade again. You might lose grandfathered pricing.

### Q: Can I use both Pro and Unlimited accounts?

**A**: No. Each GoHighLevel login has one account with one plan. You can't have a Pro account and an Unlimited account under the same email.

However, you can have multiple accounts under different emails (e.g., one for your agency, one for a white-label client). This is less common but possible for advanced use cases.
