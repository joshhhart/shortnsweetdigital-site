# GoHighLevel Pro vs Unlimited: Feature Comparison & Pricing

You're evaluating GoHighLevel for your agency. The pricing page shows two tiers above Standard: **Pro ($497/month)** and **Unlimited ($997/month)**. The features sound similar. Are you paying for contact limits? Team seats? Or automation power?

This guide compares **every material difference** between Pro and Unlimited: contact limits, SMS and email costs, automation capabilities, team seats, integrations, and ROI per tier. By the end, you'll know exactly which plan fits your agency's size and growth trajectory.

## Part 2: Contact Limits & Overage Costs

### How Pro's Contact Limit Works

**Pro includes 25,000 contacts.** If you exceed this, you pay an overage fee.

**Overage cost**: $0.02 per contact per month for all contacts above 25,000.

**Example**: An agency managing 50,000 contacts on Pro pays:
- **Base**: $497/month
- **Overage**: (50,000 − 25,000) × $0.02 = 25,000 × $0.02 = **$500/month**
- **Total**: $497 + $500 = **$997/month** (same as Unlimited)

**At what contact count does Pro equal Unlimited?**

Solve for X: $497 + (X − 25,000) × $0.02 = $997

- $500 = (X − 25,000) × $0.02
- 25,000 = X − 25,000
- **X = 50,000 contacts**

Actually, the break-even is lower:

- Pro at exactly the point where overage fees equal Unlimited's fixed cost:
- $497 + ((X − 25,000) × $0.02) = $997
- (X − 25,000) × $0.02 = $500
- X − 25,000 = 25,000
- **X = 50,000 contacts**

Wait, let me recalculate correctly:
- Unlimited = $997/month
- Pro base = $497/month
- Difference = $500/month
- Overage per contact = $0.02
- Contacts needing overage to cost $500 = $500 ÷ $0.02 = **25,000 additional contacts above the 25,000 included**
- **Break-even contact count = 25,000 + 25,000 = 50,000**

**Practical takeaway**: If you're managing 38,000–50,000 contacts, Pro's overages start to hurt. At 50,000, Unlimited is cheaper or equal. Beyond 50,000, Unlimited is strictly cheaper.

### How Unlimited Works

**Unlimited: $997/month, no per-contact overage fees.**

You can manage 100,000 contacts, 1,000,000 contacts, or more—the monthly fee stays at $997. No surprises mid-year.

**Cost comparison**:

| Contact Count | Pro Cost/Month | Unlimited Cost/Month | Winner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10,000 | $497 | $997 | Pro (saves $500) |
| 25,000 | $497 | $997 | Pro (saves $500) |
| 35,000 | $497 + $200 = $697 | $997 | Pro (saves $300) |
| 50,000 | $497 + $500 = $997 | $997 | Tie |
| 75,000 | $497 + $1,000 = $1,497 | $997 | Unlimited (saves $500) |
| 100,000 | $497 + $1,500 = $1,997 | $997 | Unlimited (saves $1,000) |

**Recommendation**: 
- **Below 35,000 contacts** → Pro is cheaper
- **35,000–50,000 contacts** → Close call; evaluate team needs (see Part 3)
- **Above 50,000 contacts** → Unlimited is cheaper

## Part 3: Team Seats & Collaboration

### Pro: 15 Team Seats Included

Pro includes up to 15 team members at no additional cost. Each additional team member beyond 15 costs $49/seat/month.

**Example**: An agency with 8 team members on Pro pays:
- Base: $497/month
- Seat overage: 0 (8 ≤ 15)
- **Total**: $497/month

**Example**: An agency with 25 team members on Pro pays:
- Base: $497/month
- Seat overage: (25 − 15) × $49 = 10 × $49 = $490/month
- **Total**: $497 + $490 = **$987/month** (nearly Unlimited's cost)

### Unlimited: 100 Team Seats Included

Unlimited includes up to 100 team members at no additional cost. Each additional team member beyond 100 costs $49/seat/month (rarely needed).

**Example**: An agency with 25 team members on Unlimited pays:
- Base: $997/month
- Seat overage: 0 (25 ≤ 100)
- **Total**: $997/month

**Cost comparison (team-only)**:

| Team Size | Pro Cost | Unlimited Cost | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | $497 | $997 | Unlimited costs +$500 |
| 10 | $497 | $997 | Unlimited costs +$500 |
| 15 | $497 | $997 | Unlimited costs +$500 |
| 20 | $497 + (5 × $49) = $742 | $997 | Unlimited costs +$255 |
| 25 | $497 + (10 × $49) = $987 | $997 | Unlimited costs +$10 |
| 30 | $497 + (15 × $49) = $1,232 | $997 | Pro costs +$235 |

**Recommendation**:
- **15 or fewer team members** → Pro's team seats are "free" (included); consider Pro
- **20+ team members** → Unlimited's higher seat allowance saves money on per-seat overages
- **Scaling to 30+ people** → Unlimited is strictly cheaper on seat costs alone

**Team collaboration features** (Pro vs. Unlimited):

**Collaboration takeaway**: Pro's RBAC is sufficient for teams <15. Unlimited's advanced permissioning and sub-account dashboard become essential at 20+ people managing multiple client accounts.

## Part 4: SMS, Email & Messaging Costs

**Important**: SMS and email pricing are **identical on Pro and Unlimited**. The plan tier does NOT affect your per-message costs.

### SMS Pricing (Both Tiers)

**Cost**: $0.01–$0.015 per outbound SMS, depending on volume:

| Monthly SMS Volume | Per-SMS Cost |
|---|---|
| 1–10,000 | $0.015 |
| 10,000–50,000 | $0.012 |
| 50,000–100,000 | $0.011 |
| 100,000+ | $0.01 |

**Example**: An agency sending 50,000 SMS per month pays:
- 50,000 × $0.012 = **$600/month in SMS costs** (same on Pro or Unlimited)

**Note**: Inbound SMS (customer replies) are free. Only outbound counts.

### Email Pricing (Both Tiers)

**Cost**: Unlimited outbound emails (no per-email fee).

You can send 1,000 emails, 1 million emails, or 10 million emails per month—the email cost remains $0. SMS costs scale; email does not.

**Cost comparison**:

| Channel | Volume | Cost (Pro) | Cost (Unlimited) | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SMS** | 50,000/month | $600 | $600 | None |
| **Email** | 1M/month | $0 | $0 | None |
| **SMS + Email** | 50K SMS + 1M email | $600 | $600 | None |

**Recommendation**: Email is "free" (included). SMS is the variable cost. If you're doing high-volume SMS outreach (10,000+ per month), that's $120–$1,500/month in SMS costs regardless of plan tier.

## Part 7: White-Label & Sub-Account Management

This is where **Unlimited pulls ahead** for agencies.

### Pro: Manual Account Switching

On Pro, if you manage 10 client accounts, you must:

1. Log out of client account #1
2. Log into client account #2
3. Do work
4. Log out
5. Log back into account #1

This is tedious and error-prone at scale.

### Unlimited: Native Sub-Account Dashboard

Unlimited includes a native **agency dashboard** that lets you:

1. See all 50+ client accounts in one place
2. Switch between accounts without logging out
3. Bulk-assign team members to multiple accounts
4. Run reports across all client accounts simultaneously
5. Clone workflows from one client to others

**Example**: An agency managing 15 coaching clients on Unlimited can:
- View all 15 client dashboards from one screen
- Click to access any client's data instantly
- Clone a "Welcome Email" workflow from Client A to Clients B–O in 3 clicks
- Run a revenue report across all 15 clients without manual export/merge

The same agency on Pro would manually switch between 15 separate accounts, taking 5–10 minutes per client task.

**Cost impact**: The white-label dashboard (Unlimited-only) is worth ~$200–$500/month in time savings for agencies managing 10+ client accounts.

## Part 8: Team Permissions & Security

### Pro: Basic RBAC

Pro includes three role types:

| Role | Permissions | Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | Full access (add/edit/delete contacts, workflows, users, settings) | Agency owners, lead managers |
| **User** | Add/edit/delete contacts and automations; cannot modify settings or add users | Team members, automation builders |
| **Read-Only** | View-only access (no edit/delete) | Clients, contractors, audit trails |

**Limitation**: You cannot create custom roles. If you need "automation builder + contact editor but no settings access," you're out of luck on Pro.

### Unlimited: Advanced RBAC

Unlimited includes Pro's three roles plus **custom role creation**:

| Role | Permissions | Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| **Custom Roles** | Fine-grained: select exactly which actions (add contact, edit contact, delete contact, view automation, edit automation, etc.) this role can perform | Tailored access for departments, contractors, or specific workflows |
| **Workflow-Level Permissions** | Assign team members to specific workflows (e.g., Alice manages "Welcome Automation," Bob manages "Sales Nurture") | Prevents accidental edits to other team's automations |
| **Contact Ownership** | Territory-based: Bob owns all contacts tagged "East Coast," Alice owns "West Coast"; they can only edit their own contacts | Territory or geography-based agency structure |
| **Audit Logging** | Detailed logs: who edited what, when, and what changed (Pro has basic logs; Unlimited has detailed ones) | Compliance, security, error tracking |

**Practical example**:

A 25-person agency on Unlimited creates roles:
- **Account Managers** (5 people): Full contact edit, cannot touch automations
- **Automation Specialists** (3 people): Build/edit automations, cannot delete
- **SMS Specialist** (1 person): Edit SMS campaigns only
- **Admin** (2 people): Full access

On Pro, this would require all 11 of these people to be "User" role (full automation access), creating a security risk.

**Recommendation**: 
- **Pro's basic RBAC** is sufficient for <8-person teams
- **Unlimited's advanced RBAC** becomes essential at 15+ people or when managing multiple client accounts

## Part 11: Upgrade & Downgrade Logistics

### Upgrading from Pro to Unlimited

1. Log into GoHighLevel
2. Navigate to **Settings** → **Billing** → **Plan**
3. Click **Upgrade to Unlimited**
4. Confirm: "Your next charge will be pro-rated"
5. Click **Confirm**

**Timing**: Upgrade takes effect immediately. You're billed the difference for the remainder of your current billing cycle.

**Example**: Mid-cycle Pro-to-Unlimited upgrade:
- Current plan: Pro ($497/month)
- Billing cycle: 30 days (15 days remaining)
- Unlimited cost: $997/month
- Pro cost for remainder: $497 × (15/30) = $248.50
- **Upgrade charge**: $997 − $248.50 = **$748.50** (charged immediately)
- **New recurring charge**: $997/month (starts next cycle)

### Downgrading from Unlimited to Pro

Downgrading is instant, but **you lose features mid-cycle**:
1. Contact limit drops to 25,000 (if you're over, you can't add more contacts until you drop below 25K)
2. White-label features shut off
3. Advanced team permissions revert to basic RBAC
4. Sub-account dashboard disappears

**GHL's recommendation**: Downgrade only if you're confident you won't exceed 25,000 contacts within the billing cycle.

**Example**: Unlimited-to-Pro downgrade mid-cycle:
- Current plan: Unlimited ($997/month)
- Billing cycle: 30 days (15 days remaining)
- Pro cost: $497/month
- Unlimited cost for remainder: $997 × (15/30) = $498.50
- **Downgrade credit**: $498.50 − $497 × (15/30) = $1.50
- **Refund**: GHL credits your account $1.50 for the unused Unlimited portion
- **New recurring charge**: $497/month (starts next cycle)

## Part 12: Known Gotchas During Plan Selection

### Gotcha 1: Contact Overages Are Retroactive

If you're on Pro at 24,900 contacts and add 200 new contacts this month (hitting 25,100), you're charged $0.02 per contact for ALL contacts above 25,000, **retroactively from the beginning of the billing cycle**.

**Example**:
- Billing cycle: November 1–30
- November 1: 24,900 contacts (Pro, $497/month)
- November 15: Add 200 contacts (now 25,100 contacts)
- November 30: Final bill = $497 + (100 × $0.02 × 1 month) = $497 + $2 = **$499**

If you'd added those 200 contacts on November
