# White-Label Client Contract Templates for Agencies: GHL Edition

You're reselling GoHighLevel to clients. You've set your pricing, trained your team, and you're ready to onboard the first paying customer. Then the question hits: **What contract do they sign?**

Most agencies skip this step or use a generic SaaS agreement that doesn't fit white-label resale. The result: ambiguous support expectations, payment disputes, unclear overage terms, and messy cancellations.

This post includes **three production-ready contract templates** for different client scenarios:

1. **Starter Agreement** — for solo coaches and small service providers (simple, 2 pages)
2. **Standard Service Agreement** — for small agencies and scaling practices (comprehensive, 4 pages)
3. **Enterprise White-Label Agreement** — for high-touch resell partnerships (detailed, 6 pages)

Each template covers pricing, support tiers, contact overages, data access, termination, and liability. All three are based on real GoHighLevel reseller deployments and have been tested with 80+ agencies.

- *Have a qualified attorney licensed in your jurisdiction review the agreement*
- *Customize terms to match your specific business, support model, and pricing*
- *Ensure compliance with local consumer protection, SaaS, and data privacy laws*
- *Disclose any affiliate relationships or commission structures (if applicable)*

*We are not attorneys. We have no liability for contracts that use these templates without proper legal review. Use at your own risk.*

> **Key Takeaways**
> - **Three templates provided**: Starter (2 pages, $299–$499/mo clients), Standard (4 pages, $499–$799/mo clients), Enterprise (6 pages, $800+/mo or resell partners)
> - **Core clauses all three templates include**: Service description, pricing + overages, support SLAs, data access, termination rights, liability limits, confidentiality
> - **Support tier SLAs**: Starter = email-only, 48-hour response; Standard = monthly check-in + Slack + 24-hour response; Enterprise = dedicated support manager + phone + weekly check-in
> - **Contact overages**: Pro plan covers 500 contacts; $1.75/contact/month overage applies; templates clarify who pays (you or client) and when overages are billed
> - **Payment terms**: Net 30 days standard; annual prepay discounts (typically 10–15%) encouraged to improve cash flow
> - **Data access & export**: Client owns their data; templates include 30-day data export window upon termination; no hostage data
> - **Termination clauses**: Month-to-month (can cancel anytime with 30 days' notice); annual contracts (prepay discount but 30-day early-termination fee applies)
> - **Non-compete & confidentiality**: Mild non-compete (client cannot directly contact your contacts/leads without permission for 6 months post-termination); standard NDA for both parties
> - **Liability cap**: Typical cap is 12 months of fees paid; excludes IP infringement, data breach, and willful misconduct
> - **Customization checklist**: 15-point review list ensures you adapt templates to your specific pricing model, support hours, jurisdiction, and affiliate disclosures
> - **Download formats**: All three templates available as editable Word (.docx) files; customization guide included

4.4 **No Data Backup After Termination**. Reseller does not back up or retain Client's data post-termination. It is Client's responsibility to export and store data before the 30-day window closes.

5.1 **Term**. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues on a month-to-month basis unless terminated by either party.

5.2 **Cancellation by Client**. Client may cancel at any time by providing Reseller with 30 days' written notice via email. The final month's fee is due in full.

5.3 **Cancellation by Reseller**. Reseller may cancel if Client breaches the Agreement (e.g., non-payment for 30+ days) or violates any terms. Reseller will provide 30 days' notice unless the breach is material (e.g., illegal use of the Service).

5.4 **Effect of Termination**. Upon termination:
