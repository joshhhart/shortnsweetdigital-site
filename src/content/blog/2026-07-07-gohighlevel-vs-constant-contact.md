# GoHighLevel vs Constant Contact: Which CRM Wins for Agencies in 2026?

You're choosing between two platforms. GoHighLevel promises an all-in-one suite for sales, email, SMS, and client management. Constant Contact focuses on email marketing and small-business contact management with a lighter feature set.

Both claim to save agencies time. Both offer integrations and automation. But they're built for different types of businesses, and picking the wrong one wastes months and budget.

This guide compares them head-to-head across 8 dimensions: features, pricing, ease of use, integrations, automation depth, white-label options, customer support, and best-fit use cases. By the end, you'll know exactly which platform matches your agency's needs.

> **Key Takeaways**
> - GoHighLevel ($497/month Unlimited) includes CRM + email + SMS + sales pipelines + landing pages + calendar; Constant Contact ($20–$335/month, 2026 pricing) focuses on email with basic CRM; GHL is 5–10x more full-featured ([GoHighLevel, 2026](https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing))
> - For agencies with clients (white-label, reseller model): GoHighLevel wins decisively; Constant Contact lacks sub-account architecture
> - For small-business owners selling direct (B2B services, consulting, local): Constant Contact's email-first approach is faster to master; GHL's learning curve is steep
> - Price-per-feature: GHL costs ~$1/feature/month; Constant Contact costs ~$3–5/feature/month; GHL only makes economic sense with 5+ client accounts or 500+ contacts ([analysis based on 2026 feature audits](https://www.seerinteractive.com))
> - Migration risk: switching platforms mid-year (contacts, automations, history) takes 6–8 weeks; plan migration for Q1 or Q4 to avoid operational chaos

## TL;DR: Quick Answer (For the Impatient)

**GoHighLevel wins** if you're an agency managing multiple client accounts, need white-label branding, want SMS + email + sales automation in one platform, and can justify $497/month for unlimited features.

**Constant Contact wins** if you're a solo consultant or small business, rely heavily on email marketing, need a simpler interface with less setup, and budget is under $100/month.

If you're torn, start with Constant Contact for 30 days (no credit card required). If you outgrow it in 6 months, migrate to GoHighLevel. Most agencies find the switch necessary within a year.

**SMS automation**:
- Multi-step SMS sequences
- Conditional branching (same as email)
- 2-way SMS (reply handling)
- Trigger types: Same as email + SMS reply

**CRM workflows**:
- Contact created → auto-assign to rep → send welcome email → schedule follow-up task
- Deal stage change → send email to customer → post Slack alert → create invoice
- Contact tagged "high-value" → re-assign to senior rep → send personalized email

**Example automation** (complex, multi-channel):
```
Trigger: Contact fills out "Book Demo" form
↓
Step 1: Add tag "Demo Requested"
Step 2: Send SMS: "Thanks for booking! We'll send calendar link in 1 min"
Step 3: Wait 1 minute
Step 4: Send Email: "Here's your calendar link [LINK]"
Step 5: Create task: "Sales rep: follow up in 24h if no meeting scheduled"
Step 6: IF meeting not scheduled within 24 hours:
         → Send email: "Ready to chat about [PRODUCT]?"
         → Wait 3 days
         → Send SMS: "Still interested? Reply YES to schedule"
         → Wait 7 days
         → Move contact to "Cold Lead" list
ELSE:
         → Send email: "Great! See you at [TIME]"
```

**Power users**: GHL supports this. Easy.

### Constant Contact Automation Depth

**Email automation**:
- Multi-step sequences (up to ~10 steps practical limit)
- Conditional branching (simpler than GHL; if/then only)

- Trigger types: New contact, contact tagged, contact unsubscribes, email opened/clicked
- Limited dynamic content support

**SMS automation** (Premium+ only):
- Single SMS or basic sequences (not as powerful as email)
- Conditional logic: Yes/No responses only
- Limited integration with email workflows

**CRM workflows**:
- Contact created → send welcome email
- Contact tagged → trigger email sequence
- Limited beyond email trigger chains

**Example automation** (simpler):

## Migration Guide: Switching Platforms

![Migration Guide: Switching Platforms](/images/2026-07-07-gohighlevel-vs-constant-contact-s2.jpg)


### From Constant Contact → GoHighLevel

**Timeline**: 6–8 weeks

**Phase 1: Audit** (Week 1)
- Inventory your Constant Contact data:
  - Contact count and custom fields
  - Active email sequences (count and complexity)
  - Landing pages (count and performance)
  - SMS campaigns (if Premium+)
- Document: Which sequences and pages are active? Which are archived?
- Export: All contacts as CSV from Constant Contact

**Phase 2: Setup** (Weeks 2–3)
- Create GoHighLevel Unlimited account ($497)
- Connect payment processor (Stripe/PayPal)
- Build white-label branding (custom domain, logo, colors)
- Import contacts into GHL (CSV upload; takes 30 min)
- Test contact sync (verify all fields imported correctly)

**Phase 3: Rebuild Automations** (Weeks 4–5)
- Replicate active email sequences in GHL
- Build equivalent SMS automation (if Premium+ Constant Contact)
- Test each sequence with test contacts
- Validate delivery and open rates (A/B compare vs old platform)

**Phase 4: Rebuild Landing Pages** (Weeks 5–6)
- Recreate high-performing landing pages in GHL
- Redirect old Constant Contact landing page URLs → new GHL URLs (301 redirect)
- Update form embed codes on your website

**Phase 5: Launch & Monitor** (Weeks 7–8)
- Pause old Constant Contact automations
- Activate GHL automations
- Run in parallel for 1 week (safety net)
- Monitor deliverability, open rates, click rates
- Deactivate Constant Contact; cancel subscription

**Gotchas**:
- Constant Contact → GHL custom field mapping: Not automatic; requires manual mapping
- Email address + contact history: Transfers fine
- Automations are not portable; must rebuild
- Landing pages require manual rebuild (no export/import)

**Cost of migration**: 80–120 hours of labor. If you're paying yourself $50/hour, that's $4,000–6,000 in time investment.

### From GoHighLevel → Constant Contact (Reverse)

**Much harder.** GHL features (white-label, sales pipelines, SMS + email bundling) don't have equivalents in CC.

- Contact export: Easy
- Automation export: Not possible (manual rebuild of email sequences only)
- Landing pages: Not portable
- Sales pipeline: No equivalent in CC (data is lost)

**Not recommended** unless you're downsizing significantly (cost-cutting).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use both platforms together?**

A: Yes. Some agencies use Constant Contact for email/SMS and GHL for CRM/sales pipeline. Zapier bridges them. But this creates sync nightmares. Not recommended unless your email and CRM are completely separate (rare).

**Q: If I start with Constant Contact and outgrow it, how hard is the switch?**

A: Moderate. Contacts export easily (5 min). Automations must rebuild in GHL (10–20 hours if you have 10+ sequences). Landing pages rebuild (10–15 hours). Total effort: 2–3 weeks of part-time work. Doable.

**Q: Does GHL offer a free trial?**

A: Yes. 14 days free (no credit card required). Test white-label setup and automations before committing to $497.

**Q: Which platform is better for e-commerce (Shopify/WooCommerce)?**

A: Tie. Both integrate with Shopify and WooCommerce. GHL is better if you want post-purchase automation (upsells via SMS + email). CC is better if you're email/SMS focused and want simplicity.

**Q: Can I white-label Constant Contact?**

A: No. Some resellers create a wrapper interface (custom domain, branded emails) using Zapier + other tools. It's a hack and not recommended.

**Q: What's the migration path if I choose wrong platform?**

A: **Constant Contact → GHL**: 6–8 weeks, doable. **GHL → CC**: Not recommended (lose sales pipeline and white-label). Plan carefully before choosing.
