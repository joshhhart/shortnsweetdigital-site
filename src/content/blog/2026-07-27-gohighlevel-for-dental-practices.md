## The Problem: No-Shows and Lost Treatment Cases

### No-Shows Cost Real Money

Imagine you run a general dentistry practice with 35 appointments per week. Your average appointment value is $250 (mix of cleanings, fillings, root canals, cosmetic cases).

**Current state (no automation)**:
- 35 appointments/week × 4 weeks = 140 appointments/month
- No-show rate: 12% (industry average for practices without automated reminders)
- 140 × 0.12 = **16.8 no-shows per month**
- Lost revenue: 16.8 × $250 = **$4,200/month**
- Annual lost revenue: $4,200 × 12 = **$50,400/year**

That's equivalent to one full-time operatory sitting idle.

### Treatment Acceptance Is Leaving Money on the Table

You present a $3,000 crown or a $5,000 implant case. Patient says, "Let me think about it." You send a printed estimate. Patient goes home, never reads it, and calls another practice for a second opinion.

**Current state (no follow-up automation)**:
- 20 treatment plans presented per month
- Acceptance rate (without follow-up): 40% (8 cases accepted immediately)
- Lost cases due to no follow-up: 12 cases × $3,000 average = **$36,000/month in lost cases**

If you could increase acceptance to 55% (via automated treatment plan reminders and follow-up), that's 4 additional cases/month × $3,000 = **$12,000/month in recovered revenue**.

### Staff Time Spent on Reminders

Your front desk staff member spends 2 hours per day making reminder calls.

- 2 hours/day × 22 business days/month = **44 hours/month**
- At $18/hour (front desk rate): **$792/month** in labor
- That's labor that could go toward patient interactions instead

## Phased Setup Guide: 6 Weeks to Full Automation

Don't implement everything at once. Roll out in phases to give staff time to learn and optimize workflows.

### Week 1: Planning and Integration

**Goal**: Decide what to automate and set up GoHighLevel + practice software integration.

**Phase 1a: Audit Current Workflows (2 hours)**
- Front desk staff: Document how reminder calls are currently made (when, who, how many)
- Dentists/hygienists: Document how treatment plans are currently presented and followed up
- Example questions:
  - When do we send appointment reminders? (day-before, day-of, etc.)
  - How many patients confirm vs. don't answer?
  - How often do patients cancel the day-of?
  - Do we follow up with patients who decline treatment plans?

**Phase 1b: Choose Which Workflows to Automate (1 hour)**
- Start with appointment reminders (biggest impact, easiest to implement)
- Later (week 3+): Add treatment follow-up, referral requests, re-care reminders

**Phase 1c: Create GoHighLevel Account and Connect Practice Software (2–4 hours)**
- Sign up for GoHighLevel Professional plan ($199/month) at https://www.gohighlevel.com/?fp_ref=shortnsweet53
- Follow integration guide for your practice software (Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Curve, etc.)
- Test one appointment sync to confirm it works
- **Troubleshooting tip**: If integration fails, check that your practice software's API key is correct and that Zapier account has sufficient actions remaining.

**Deliverables by end of week 1**:
- Written workflow plan (which workflows, in what order)
- GoHighLevel account set up
- Practice software connected (test sync confirmed)

### Week 2: First Workflow - Appointment Reminders

**Goal**: Launch automated appointment reminders (SMS 48h before + Email 24h before).

**Phase 2a: Set Up SMS Workflow in GoHighLevel (1 hour)**
1. Go to GoHighLevel → Automations → Create New Workflow
2. Trigger: "Appointment created in Zapier sync" OR "Contact added to 'Upcoming Appointments' segment"
3. Actions:
   - **SMS 48 hours before appointment**: "Hi [Patient Name], your appointment is on [Date] at [Time]. Reply YES to confirm."
   - **SMS 24 hours before**: "Reminder: Your appointment tomorrow at [Time]. See you then!"
4. Test with a staff member's phone (send yourself the SMS)

**Phase 2b: Set Up Email Workflow (30 min)**
1. Create email template: "Appointment Reminder - [Date]"
2. Add template to same workflow (trigger: 24 hours before)
3. Test: Send email to yourself; verify formatting and links

**Phase 2c: Launch to Live Patients (30 min)**
- Activate workflow
- Monitor first 24 hours: Check delivery rates, see if patients reply

**Phase 2d: Monitor and Optimize (ongoing)**
- Track: How many SMS delivered? How many bounced? (typical: 95%+ delivery)
- Track: How many patients confirmed? (typical: 60–80%)
- Adjust timing if needed (e.g., if SMS 48h before doesn't work, try 72h)

**Deliverables by end of week 2**:
- Appointment reminder workflows live
- SMS and email templates created
- First metrics captured (delivery rate, confirmation rate)

**Expected result**: Week 2 has low impact (only 1 week of data). Expect to see impact by week 3–4 as workflow runs consistently.

- Track: How many contacts tagged? How many scheduled after follow-up?
- Example: If 20 plans tagged/month and 5 schedule after follow-up (when 0 would have before), that's +25% improvement

**Deliverables by end of week 3**:
- Treatment plan follow-up workflow live

- Baseline metrics captured
