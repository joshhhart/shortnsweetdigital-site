## What Are Automation Workflows? (And Why They Matter)

Automation workflows are conditional sequences that run when a trigger event happens. No manual action needed.

**Simple example**:

```
TRIGGER: Form submitted (new lead)
↓
ACTION 1: Send welcome email immediately
↓
ACTION 2: Wait 2 hours
↓
ACTION 3: Send SMS with booking link
↓
ACTION 4: If link clicked → add tag "Interested" and schedule sales call
↓
ACTION 5: If link not clicked within 24 hours → send follow-up email
```

**Without automation** (manual work):
- Lead submits form
- You receive notification
- You manually send email ("thanks for inquiring")
- 2 hours later, you remember to send SMS
- You log into calendar tool to schedule call
- You send calendar link
- You wait for confirmation
- **Time spent**: 15-20 minutes per lead

**With automation** (hands-off):
- Lead submits form
- GoHighLevel runs the sequence automatically
- You only interact when the lead books a call (pre-qualified)
- **Time spent**: 0 minutes per lead (except handling qualified leads)

**Math for 30 new leads per month**:
- **Manual**: 30 leads × 20 min = 600 minutes (10 hours/month)
- **Automated**: 30 leads × 2 min (only booking qualified leads) = 60 minutes (1 hour/month)
- **Time saved**: 9 hours/month = 108 hours/year

At $50/hour billable rate, this automation saves $5,400/year in labor. At $100/hour, it saves $10,800/year.

## The 12 Must-Have Automation Workflows

### Workflow 1: Lead Welcome Sequence (5-Minute Nurture)

**When to use**: Every new lead that submits a form or is manually added to CRM.

**Trigger**: Contact added (via form or manual entry)

**Sequence**:

1. **Send welcome email** (immediate)
   - Subject: "Welcome to [Agency]—Here's Your Next Step"
   - Body: Your unique positioning + what they'll get from working with you (2-3 sentences) + link to book 15-min consultation

2. **Wait 2 hours**

3. **Send SMS** (if phone present)
   - Message: "Hi [Name]! Thanks for reaching out. Book your 15-min consultation here: [Link]. Anytime this week works for us."

4. **Wait 24 hours** (total 26 hours from initial trigger)

5. **If booking link clicked**: Jump to Workflow 3 (Appointment Confirmation)

6. **If booking link NOT clicked**: Send follow-up email
   - Subject: "Quick question—what's holding you back?"
   - Body: "I noticed you haven't booked yet. What's stopping you? [Reply to this email, I'll help.]"

**Setup time**: 5 minutes

**Impact**: 40-60% of leads who receive sequence book (vs. 10-15% with no follow-up)

### Workflow 2: Demo or Free Trial Signup Sequence (5-Minute Setup)

**When to use**: Someone signs up for a free trial or demo of your software/service.

**Trigger**: Free trial started OR Demo booked

**Sequence**:

1. **Send welcome email** (immediate)
   - Subject: "Your [Product] Demo Starts Now—Here's Your Login"
   - Include: demo credentials, 3-min quickstart video link, support phone number

2. **Wait 1 day**

3. **Send SMS** (if phone present)
   - Message: "Hi [Name]! How's the [Product] demo going? Any questions? Call/text anytime: [Phone]"

4. **Wait 2 days** (total 3 days from start)

5. **If NOT logged in yet**: Send reminder email
   - Subject: "Get started with [Product] in 10 minutes—I'll walk you through it"
   - Body: "Looks like you haven't logged in yet. Want a quick walkthrough call? [Book 10-min call]"

6. **Wait 2 days** (total 5 days from start)

7. **Send check-in SMS**
   - Message: "Demo ending soon! What questions can I answer? [Phone]"

8. **Wait 2 days** (total 7 days from start)

9. **If trial ending AND no conversion**: Send last-chance email
   - Subject: "Last 24 hours—lock in [Special Offer] pricing"
   - Body: "Your [Product] trial ends tomorrow. If you'd like to continue, we can get you set up in 10 minutes. [Buy Now] or [Schedule Call]"

**Setup time**: 7 minutes

**Impact**: 25-40% of trial signups convert to paid (vs. 5-10% with no follow-up)

### Workflow 3: Appointment Confirmation & Reminder (3-Minute Setup)

**When to use**: After every appointment is booked (via GoHighLevel calendar or external calendar sync).

**Trigger**: Appointment created

**Sequence**:

1. **Send confirmation email** (immediate)
   - Subject: "Confirmed: Your [Meeting Type] on [Date] at [Time]"
   - Include: meeting details, location/Zoom link, what to prepare, cancellation policy

2. **Wait until 24 hours before appointment**

3. **Send SMS reminder** (24 hours before)
   - Message: "Hi [Name], reminder: your [Meeting Type] is tomorrow at [Time] at [Location]. Reply CONFIRM or click to reschedule: [Link]"

4. **Wait until 1 hour before appointment**

5. **Send second SMS reminder** (1 hour before)
   - Message: "Your appointment is in 1 hour at [Location]. See you soon!"

6. **If client replies CONFIRM**: Nothing (meeting proceeds)

7. **If client replies RESCHEDULE**: Add tag "Reschedule Request" (for manual follow-up) + send rescheduling link

8. **If client doesn't show up**: Send post-appointment SMS
   - Message: "We missed you at your appointment today. Call [Phone] to reschedule—no penalty."

**Setup time**: 3 minutes

**Impact**: 40-60% reduction in no-shows (ISSA, 2026)

### Workflow 4: Lead Qualification (Multi-Step Scoring)

**When to use**: Separate high-value leads from low-value leads automatically.

**Trigger**: Lead form submitted

**Sequence**:

1. **Add initial tag** "Hot Lead" (temporary)

2. **Ask qualification questions** (via email or SMS)
   - Email: "Quick 30-second question: What's your [main pain point]?"
   - Options: [Cost], [Time], [Quality], [Complexity]

3. **Wait for response** (max 2 days)

4. **Score based on response**:
   - If pain point = "Cost" (price-sensitive, lower priority): Remove "Hot Lead" tag, add "Budget Lead"
   - If pain point = "Quality" or "Complexity" (higher-value, willing to pay): Keep "Hot Lead" tag, add "Sales Priority"

5. **For "Sales Priority" leads**: Trigger Workflow 5 (Sales Sequence)

6. **For "Budget Lead"**: Trigger Workflow 6 (Value-Focused Sequence)

**Setup time**: 8 minutes

**Impact**: Sales team focuses on high-value leads first (3x conversion rate)

### Workflow 5: Sales Follow-Up Sequence (7-Minute Setup)

**When to use**: Qualified leads who haven't booked yet (from Workflow 4 or manual tag).

**Trigger**: Tag "Sales Priority" added

**Sequence**:

1. **Send initial sales email** (immediate)
   - Subject: "Here's how [Agency] helped [Similar Company] increase revenue by [%]"
   - Body: Case study excerpt + link to book 30-min sales call

2. **Wait 1 day**

3. **Send SMS** (if phone present)
   - Message: "Hi [Name]! Did you see the case study I sent? We helped [Company] achieve [Result]. Think we could do the same for you? [Book Call]"

4. **Wait 2 days**

5. **Send second sales email**
   - Subject: "3 mistakes [Industry] companies make—and how to fix them"
   - Body: Educational email (not salesy) + [Book Strategy Call] CTA

6. **Wait 3 days**

7. **Send final SMS** (last attempt before downgrade)
   - Message: "Last attempt! [Agency] is offering [Special Offer] this week only. Ready to talk? [Book Now]"

8. **If booking link clicked**: Remove from sequence, proceed to Workflow 3 (Appointment Confirmation)

9. **If no response after 7 days**: Add tag "Nurture" (move to long-term nurture, see Workflow 10)

**Setup time**: 7 minutes

**Impact**: 25-35% of qualified leads convert to paid customers (vs. 8-12% with single email)

### Workflow 6: Cold Email Follow-Up (Batch)

**When to use**: Cold outreach list (LinkedIn exports, lead database purchases, or custom list).

**Trigger**: Batch "Cold Email Campaign" (or tag "Cold Prospect")

**Sequence**:

1. **Send cold email #1** (day 1)
   - Subject: Short, personalized line (e.g., "Quick question about [their company]")
   - Body: 3 sentences max. No pitch. Ask a question or offer value.
   - Example: "Hi [FirstName], saw you're using [Competitor]. We help [Industry] companies improve [Metric] by [%]. Worth a 10-min chat? [Calendar]"

2. **Wait 3 days**

3. **Send cold email #2** (day 4)
   - Subject: "Re: Quick question about [their company]" (reply-format subject)
   - Body: Different angle. Reference first email subtly. Offer value again.

4. **Wait 3 days**

5. **Send cold SMS** (day 7, if phone present)
   - Message: "Hi [FirstName], sent you a couple emails. Not a fit? No worries. If you do want to chat, here's my calendar: [Link]"

6. **Wait 5 days**

7. **Send cold email #3** (day 12, final attempt)
   - Subject: "Last try—[Specific Value Prop]"
   - Body: Most direct pitch. If they don't respond, remove from list.

8. **If opened any email OR clicked link**: Move to Workflow 5 (Sales Sequence)

9. **If no response**: Add to "Nurture" tag (quarterly check-ins, see Workflow 10)

**Setup time**: 10 minutes

**Impact**: 5-10% response rate on cold outreach (vs. 1-2% without follow-up)

### Workflow 7: Customer Onboarding Sequence (8-Minute Setup)

**When to use**: New customer just signed contract or made first payment.

**Trigger**: Payment received OR Deal stage = "Won"

**Sequence**:

1. **Send onboarding welcome email** (immediate)
   - Subject: "Welcome to [Company]! Here's Your Onboarding Plan"
   - Body: 30-day roadmap, assigned account manager, first meeting scheduled, next steps

2. **Send onboarding calendar link** (immediate, separate email)
   - Include: kickoff call time, agenda, pre-meeting prep checklist

3. **Wait 1 day**

4. **Send SMS reminder**
   - Message: "Welcome aboard, [Name]! Your kickoff call is [Date] at [Time]. Any questions before then? [Phone]"

5. **Wait 1 day before kickoff call**

6. **Send meeting prep email**
   - Subject: "Kickoff Call Tomorrow—Here's What to Prepare"
   - Include: agenda, 5-min prep checklist, Zoom link

7. **Day of kickoff**: Send 1-hour reminder SMS

8. **Post-kickoff call**: Send follow-up email within 24 hours
   - Subject: "Great call! Here's what we discussed"
   - Recap: action items, timeline, next milestone date

9. **Wait 1 week** (7 days post-kickoff)

10. **Send first progress email**
    - Subject: "Week 1 Update: Here's What We've Accomplished"
    - Include: completed tasks, upcoming week focus, success metrics

11. **Repeat weekly emails for 4 weeks** (Month 1 onboarding)

12. **At 30 days**: Transition to Workflow 8 (Ongoing Customer Care)

**Setup time**: 8 minutes

**Impact**: Faster time-to-value, higher customer satisfaction, lower churn

### Workflow 8: Ongoing Customer Success (Quarterly Check-ins)

**When to use**: Active customers (every quarter).

**Trigger**: Scheduled (quarterly) OR Tag "Active Customer"

**Sequence**:

1. **Send quarterly check-in email** (every 90 days)
   - Subject: "Quarterly check-in: How's [Project] going?"
   - Body: Ask about progress toward goals, identify blockers, offer support

2. **Wait 2 days for response**

3. **Send SMS reminder** (if no email response)
   - Message: "Hey [Name], checking in on [Project]. All going well? Let's jump on a quick call: [Calendar]"

4. **Wait 2 days**

5. **If issue identified**: Trigger Workflow 9 (Issue Resolution)

6. **If progress is great**: Send celebration email + upsell/additional service offer
   - Message: "Awesome results, [Name]! Since you've achieved [Milestone], here's the next opportunity: [Additional Service]"

7. **If no response**: Schedule 1:1 call (account manager reaches out manually)

**Setup time**: 5 minutes

**Impact**: Higher retention (5-10% increase), identifies upsell opportunities

### Workflow 9: Issue/Churn Prevention Sequence

**When to use**: Customer signals dissatisfaction, or engagement drops (no activity for 30+ days).

**Trigger**: Support ticket created OR No activity for 30 days

**Sequence**:

1. **Send empathy email** (immediate if ticket, or after 30-day inactivity)
   - Subject: "We want to help—what's going on?"
   - Body: Acknowledge issue, offer support, ask what's blocking them

2. **Wait 1 day for response**

3. **If no response**: Send SMS
   - Message: "Hi [Name], noticed you might be frustrated. Let's jump on a call and fix it. [Calendar]"

4. **Schedule escalated call** (with account manager, not just support)

5. **On the call**: Listen, identify root cause, offer solution (discount, additional service, workflow change)

6. **Post-call**: Send email with solution details + timeline

7. **If customer stays**: Move to Workflow 8 (ongoing success)

8. **If customer wants to leave**: Trigger Workflow 11 (Offboarding/Win-Back)

**Setup time**: 6 minutes

**Impact**: Recover 20-30% of at-risk customers, reduce churn by 15-25%

**When to use**: Leads who aren't ready to buy yet (tagged "Nurture").

**Trigger**: Tag "Nurture" added OR Quarterly schedule

**Sequence** (repeating quarterly):

1. **Send educational email #1** (month 1)
   - Subject: "[Industry] Trend Report 2026"

4. **Wait 2 weeks**

5. **Send webinar or resource offer** (month 2)
   - Subject: "Free guide: [Common Problem Solved]"
   - Body: Valuable content offer (guide, checklist, template)

6. **Wait 4 weeks**

7. **Repeat cycle** (same emails, quarterly)

8. **If link clicked or engagement detected**: Move to Workflow 5 (Sales Sequence)

9. **If no engagement after 6 months**: Archive contact (or downgrade to annual email)

**Setup time**: 8 minutes

**Impact**: 15-30% of nurtured leads convert to customers within 12 months (vs. 5% without nurture)

### Workflow 11: Win-Back / Cancellation Prevention

**When to use**: Customer requests cancellation.

**Trigger**: Customer submits cancellation request OR Support ticket = "Cancellation"

**Sequence**:

1. **Send understanding email** (immediate)
   - Subject: "Before you go—let's see if we can fix this"
   - Body: Acknowledge their concern, ask why they're leaving, offer a call

2. **Wait 1 day**

3. **Send founder/exec message** (if contact info available)
   - Subject: "I got your cancellation request"
   - Body: Personal note from founder/CEO, offer to jump on call, offer special deal (discount, custom solution)

4. **Wait 1 day**

5. **Send final SMS** (last resort)
   - Message: "[Name], we really value you. Before we process cancellation, can we chat? [Calendar]"

6. **Wait 3 days for response**

7. **If no response**: Process cancellation, add tag "Lost Customer"

8. **If conversation happens**: 
   - Offer discount (10-20% off for 3 months)
   - Or offer custom solution
   - Or offer to pause account (pause payment, keep data safe)

9. **If accepted offer**: Remove cancellation, restart Workflow 8 (Customer Success)

10. **If declines**: Process cancellation, add to win-back email list (annual re-engagement, see Workflow 12)

**Setup time**: 7 minutes

**Impact**: Recover 15-25% of cancelling customers, extend lifetime value by $5K-15K per customer

### Workflow 12: Win-Back Campaign (Annual Re-engagement)

**When to use**: Former customers who left (tagged "Lost Customer").

**Trigger**: Annual schedule (once per year)

**Sequence**:

1. **Send re-engagement email #1** (month 1)
   - Subject: "[Company] has evolved—here's what's new"
   - Body: New features, new pricing, success stories since they left

2. **Wait 2 weeks**

3. **Send re-engagement email #2** (month 1.5)
   - Subject: "We miss you—special offer inside"
   - Body: Limited-time offer (30% discount, free month, etc.)

4. **Wait 2 weeks**

5. **Send re-engagement SMS** (month 2, if phone present)
   - Message: "Hey [Name]! We'd love to have you back. Use code [CODE] for 30% off this month. [Link]"

6. **Wait 1 month**

7. **If no response**: Archive from active list (send annual email only)

8. **If clicks link**: Send personalized sales email offering account restoration

**Setup time**: 6 minutes

**Impact**: 10-15% of lost customers return within 12 months

## How to Set Up These Workflows in GoHighLevel (Step-by-Step)

**Go to Automations → Create New Automation**

**Step 1: Name Your Automation**
- Example: "Lead Welcome Sequence"

**Step 2: Add Your Trigger**
- Click "Add Trigger"
- Select trigger type:
  - "Contact added via form" (for form submissions)
  - "Contact created" (for manual adds or imports)
  - "Appointment created" (for booking events)
  - "Payment received" (for sales automations)
  - "Tag added" (for segmented automations)
  - "Tag removed"
  - "Deal stage changed"
  - "Custom webhook" (for integrations)

**Step 3: Add Your First Action**
- Click "Add Action"
- Select action type:
  - "Send Email"
  - "Send SMS"
  - "Wait" (delay)
  - "Add Tag"
  - "Remove Tag"
  - "Create Task"
  - "Assign to User"
  - "Update Contact Field"
  - "Webhook" (integrate with Zapier, Make, etc.)
  - "Delay Until" (wait until specific time/date)

**Step 4: Configure Action Details**
- Example for "Send Email":
  - Select template (or create new)
  - Choose sender email
  - Set subject, body
  - Click "Save"

**Step 5: Add Conditions (If/Then)**
- After an action, click "Add Condition"
- Example: "If email opened"
  - Then: Send next action
  - Else: Send different action

**Step 6: Add Wait/Delay**
- Click "Add Action" → "Wait"
- Set duration: "1 day" or "2 hours" or until "Next Monday at 9am"
- Click "Save"

**Step 7: Publish**
- Click "Publish" (automation goes live and starts triggering)
- Or "Save as Draft" (to test first)

**Testing**:
- Create a test contact
- Watch automation run in the "Automation History" tab
- Check that emails send, SMS messages arrive, and tags are applied correctly
