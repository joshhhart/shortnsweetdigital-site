# GoHighLevel vs Proposition: Which Platform Wins for Agencies

Choosing an agency management platform is hard. You need contact management, automations, client pipelines, team collaboration, and integrations—all without breaking the budget. **GoHighLevel (GHL)** and **Proposition** are two of the most popular choices for small-to-mid-size agencies, but they're built on different principles.

GHL is a **broad all-in-one platform**: CRM, automations, SMS, email, landing pages, appointment booking, and more. Proposition is a **lean, client-focused CRM** designed specifically for agencies managing multiple clients and projects.

This guide compares them head-to-head: pricing, core features, compliance, integrations, team workflows, and real ROI so you can decide which platform fits your agency.

> **Key Takeaways**
> - **GHL pricing**: $99–$499/month (Pro to Agency plan). Includes SMS, email, automations, white-label pages, and unlimited contacts.
> - **Proposition pricing**: $99–$299/month (Starter to Professional plan). Includes client management, project tracking, billing, and integrations; SMS/email sold separately or via integrations.
> - **Best for GHL**: Agencies wanting an all-in-one platform to manage clients AND run marketing campaigns (content marketing, lead generation, SMS campaigns).
> - **Best for Proposition**: Agencies needing a lightweight, collaboration-focused CRM with built-in project tracking and invoicing; comfortable using Zapier/Make for SMS/email.
> - **Setup time**: GHL = 8–16 hours (more features to configure); Proposition = 4–8 hours (simpler, fewer options).
> - **Integration count**: GHL has 50–70 native pre-built integrations plus 150–200+ custom Zapier/Make workflows (verified via GHL app directory, October 2026). Proposition integrates with 40+ tools natively (Zapier, Make, webhooks) plus 500+ via Zapier (limited direct integrations as of October 2026).
> - **SMS compliance**: Both platforms require you to manage consent (TCPA, GDPR, CASL). GHL is NOT BAA-signed for HIPAA. Proposition is NOT BAA-signed either; no healthcare-specific compliance offerings from either vendor.
> - **Team collaboration**: GHL excels at broadcast marketing (SMS, email campaigns to many clients); Proposition excels at project-level collaboration (shared notes, task assignment, client-specific workspaces).
> - **Ideal scenario for GHL**: You manage 5–50 client accounts, want to run SMS/email campaigns for them, and need white-label landing pages. Budget: $149–$299/month (Unlimited or Agency plan).
> - **Ideal scenario for Proposition**: You manage 10–100 client accounts, need lightweight project tracking and invoicing, and plan to layer SMS/email via Zapier or a separate SMS provider. Budget: $99–$199/month.
> - **Annual ROI example** (Agency with 20 clients, $5K average annual client value, 15% client churn reduction via better project tracking):
>   - Revenue retained: 20 × $5K × 15% = $15K/year
>   - Platform cost: $150/month × 12 = $1,800/year
>   - **Net ROI: ($15K − $1,800) / $1,800 = 733% annual ROI** (assuming tracking and communication improvements reduce client churn)
>   - Note: This assumes your churn is driven by poor communication/tracking, not service quality. See Part 4 (ROI Reality Check) for practice-dependent ranges.
> - **Known limitations**:
>   - GHL: Steep learning curve; overkill for lightweight project-focused agencies; white-label features add complexity for small teams.
>   - Proposition: No built-in SMS/email (must use Zapier/Make); limited automation compared to GHL; fewer pre-built templates.

## Part 3: Feature Depth & Workflows

### SMS & Email Automation

#### GoHighLevel SMS Workflows

**Capabilities**:
- Trigger-based SMS (contact field change, form submitted, date reached, manual send)
- Conditional branching (if contact clicked SMS, send follow-up; else wait and retry)
- Personalization with merge fields ({{FirstName}}, {{LastName}}, {{PhoneNumber}})
- Delay actions (wait 24 hours, then send next SMS)
- A/B testing (via manual segmentation; no native A/B test framework as of October 2026)
- Opt-out tracking and compliance logging

**Example workflow** (GHL):
```
Trigger: Contact submits "Lead Form"
  ↓
Action: Send SMS "Thanks for your interest! A rep will call you within 2 hours."
  ↓
Wait 2 hours
  ↓
Condition: If contact phone answered (via call log), skip; else proceed
  ↓
Action: Send SMS "Still interested? Reply YES or call 555-1234"
  ↓
Wait 24 hours
  ↓
If no reply:
  Action: Send Email with alternative offer
Else:
  Action: Send SMS confirmation + calendar link
```

#### Proposition SMS Workflows (via Zapier)

**Capabilities** (via Zapier integration):
- Trigger-based SMS (new client, project status change, task assigned)
- Limited conditional logic (depends on Zapier plan tier)
- Personalization (depends on Zapier/SMS provider integration)
- Delay actions (Zapier supports delays up to 30 days)
- No native A/B testing

**Example workflow** (Proposition + Zapier):
```
Trigger: Proposition → New project created
  ↓
Zapier: Create Twilio SMS template
  ↓
Zapier action: Send SMS to project owner
  ↓
Zapier delay: Wait 3 days
  ↓
Zapier condition: Check Proposition → Project status
  ↓
If status = "In Progress":
  Send SMS status update
Else if status = "Completed":
  Send SMS completion notification
```

**Complexity gap**: GHL is more visually intuitive for building workflows; Zapier requires mapping fields and testing integrations, which adds overhead.

### Project & Task Management

#### GHL Task Management

- Create tasks, assign to team, set due dates
- Linked to contacts/opportunities (not projects)
- Task templates for recurring workflows
- Lacks: Sprint planning, Gantt charts, time tracking, milestone planning

**GHL use case**: "Create a task for the sales rep to call this lead by tomorrow." Limited for complex project tracking.

#### Proposition Project Management

- Projects with sub-tasks, milestones, and timelines
- Assign tasks to team members; track time estimates vs. actual
- Shared project workspace (client can see progress)
- Activity feed with @mentions and comments
- Lacks: Resource allocation, dependency management (advanced PM features)

**Proposition use case**: "Build a website for this client; track design, dev, QA, and launch milestones." Natural fit for project-based agencies.

**Verdict**: **Proposition wins for project-focused agencies**. GHL's tasks are for reminders and follow-ups, not project planning.

## Part 5: Compliance & Data Security

![Part 5: Compliance & Data Security](/images/2026-10-22-gohighlevel-vs-proposition-s1.jpg)


### HIPAA & BAA Compliance

**Neither GHL nor Proposition is BAA-signed for HIPAA.** If you serve healthcare practices (doctors, dentists, vets), you cannot use either platform to store patient health information (diagnoses, medications, appointment reasons containing clinical detail).

**Workarounds**:
1. Store contact info (name, phone, email) in GHL/Proposition; keep clinical data in a BAA-compliant system (your EHR, secure messaging platform)
2. Limit form submissions to non-clinical data (e.g., "reason: annual checkup" rather than "reason: recurring migraines")
3. Ensure data transfer is encrypted (HTTPS, not email)

**Compliance checklist** (for both platforms):
- [ ] Review data processing agreement (DPA) with GHL/Proposition
- [ ] Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on all team accounts
- [ ] Limit access to contact data based on team roles
- [ ] Document data retention and deletion policies
- [ ] Monitor access logs for unauthorized access
- [ ] Have legal counsel review your specific use case before deployment

### GDPR, CCPA, & SMS Compliance (TCPA, CASL)

**Both platforms support**:
- Opt-in/opt-out tracking (TCPA, CASL, GDPR consent)
- Unsubscribe mechanisms (one-click email unsubscribe; SMS STOP reply)
- Custom fields for consent date and source

**GHL advantage**: Built-in SMS opt-out management; easier to track TCPA compliance.
**Proposition advantage**: Requires you to use Zapier/external SMS provider; requires explicit compliance management on your side.

**Verdict**: Both are compliant if you implement consent and opt-out tracking; GHL is simpler for SMS-specific compliance.

## Part 11: Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I migrate from GHL to Proposition (or vice versa)?

**Answer**: Partially. Both platforms have CSV export for contacts and data. Migration steps:
1. Export contacts from source platform (CSV format)
2. Clean data (remove duplicates, standardize phone numbers)
3. Import to target platform
4. Manually recreate automations (no automatic workflow export/import)
5. Estimated time: 2–4 hours for 100–1,000 contacts; add 8–16 hours if you have complex automations

**Automation migration**: You'll need to recreate your automations in the new platform from scratch. There's no automated migration tool.

### What if I need HIPAA compliance (BAA)?

**Answer**: Neither GHL nor Proposition is BAA-signed. Options:
1. Use a BAA-compliant alternative (Salesforce Health Cloud, Epic Practice, Athenahealth) but these are enterprise-level and expensive ($500–2,000+/month)
2. Store only non-clinical contact data in GHL/Proposition; keep health records in your EHR (BAA-compliant)
3. Ask GHL or Proposition if they offer BAA (unlikely as of October 2026; check their roadmap)

### Can I use both GHL and Proposition together?

**Answer**: Yes, but it adds complexity. Example:
