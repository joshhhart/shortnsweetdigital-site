## The HVAC Lead Problem: Why Most HVAC Companies Lose Jobs

Before we dive into GoHighLevel, let's name the problem. An HVAC lead is hot for exactly **4 hours**. After that, the customer has already called a competitor or decided to hire a different company. Here's what happens:

**Scenario 1: The Missed Call**

A homeowner's furnace stops working at 6 PM. They Google "emergency HVAC near me." Your Google My Business listing shows up #2. They call competitor #1 first. Competitor answers in 3 rings, books the job. Your phone rings at 6:07 PM—but you're already on a service call. The customer hangs up after 2 rings. You never get a callback notification. By the time you see the missed call at 8 PM, they've hired someone else. Lost job: $3,500.

**Scenario 2: The Lost Lead in Email**

A customer submits your website form at 11 AM. The form goes to a shared inbox (Gmail, Yahoo). Your admin is in a meeting. By the time she sees it at 2 PM, the lead is cold. She emails back. Customer never replies (they've already hired). Lost job: $2,500.

**Scenario 3: The Forgotten Follow-Up**

A customer calls for a quote but says "call me back Tuesday." Your technician writes it on a notepad. Tuesday comes. Nobody remembers. Customer hires a different company. Lost job: $1,500.

**The Pattern**: Every lost lead costs $1,500–$5,000. A 10-person HVAC team that loses just 2 leads per week is leaving $156,000 on the table per year.

GoHighLevel stops this by making sure:
1. **Every inbound call or form is logged and routed instantly** to the next available person
2. **Follow-ups happen automatically** (no human memory needed)
3. **Customers see professionalism** (instant text confirmations, email reminders, branded experience)

## How to Set Up GoHighLevel for Your HVAC Company (30 Minutes)

Here's a step-by-step setup guide. Even if you're not tech-savvy, this is doable.

### Phase 1: Create Your Account & Import Customers (10 minutes)

**Step 1**: Go to [gohighlevel.com](https://www.gohighlevel.com/?fp_ref=shortnsweet53) → Click "Start Free Trial" (14 days, no credit card)

**Step 2**: Fill in your company name, email, phone

**Step 3**: You're in the dashboard. Go to **Contacts** → **Import**

**Step 4**: Download your existing customer list (from Excel, Google Sheets, or your current software) as a CSV file

**Step 5**: Upload it to GoHighLevel. Map columns:
- Column A (Name) → First Name
- Column B (Phone) → Phone
- Column C (Email) → Email
- Column D (Last Service) → Custom field "Last Service Date"

**Step 6**: Click **Import**. GoHighLevel uploads your customers (usually takes 5–30 minutes).

### Phase 2: Set Up Google Business Integration (5 minutes)

When someone calls your Google Business listing, GoHighLevel logs it automatically.

**Step 1**: Go to **Settings** → **Integrations** → **Google Business**

**Step 2**: Click **Connect** → Sign in with your Google account → Authorize GoHighLevel

**Step 3**: Select your business location

**Step 4**: Enable "Call logging" and "Review notifications"

Done. Every inbound call from Google is now logged in GoHighLevel.

### Phase 3: Create Your First SMS Automation (10 minutes)

When a new lead comes in, send them an instant text.

**Step 1**: Go to **Automations** → **Create New**

**Step 2**: Name it: "New Lead Welcome Text"

**Step 3**: Set the trigger: **"New Contact Created"** or **"New Phone Call"**

**Step 4**: Add action: **"Send SMS"**

**Step 5**: Write the message:

```
Hi {{FirstName}}! Thanks for calling [Your Company]. 

We can schedule an appointment today:
- Tomorrow 10 AM
- Tomorrow 2 PM
- Another time?

Reply with your choice or call us at [Your Phone].
```

**Step 6**: Click **Save & Publish**

Now every new lead gets an instant text response. You're already ahead of 90% of HVAC companies.

### Phase 4: Connect Your Calendar (5 minutes)

**Step 1**: Go to **Settings** → **Calendar** → **Google Calendar**

**Step 2**: Click **Connect** → Authorize

**Step 3**: Select the calendar your team uses for appointments

Done. GoHighLevel now sees your availability and won't double-book.
