# GoHighLevel White Label Markup: Pricing Strategy for Agencies

You're running an agency. You've built your own tools, or you're reselling software like HubSpot, Pipedrive, or Kajabi. You've heard about GoHighLevel white-label: you rebrand GHL under your name, sell it to clients, keep the margin between what you charge and what you pay GHL.

You're asking:

- *What's a healthy markup on GHL white-label? Is 2x, 3x, or 5x better?*
- *How much should I charge clients: $299/month, $499/month, $999/month?*
- *What's the profit per client after overhead (support, onboarding, hosting)?*
- *How do competitors price GHL white-label (HighLevel alternatives, custom CRM resellers)?*
- *What pricing psychology tricks work to justify higher markups?*
- *How do I scale from 10 white-label clients to 100 while staying profitable?*

This guide answers all of these with real case studies, pricing tables, and formulas you can adapt to your agency's cost structure.

---

> **Key Takeaways**
> - **GHL white-label cost baseline**: GHL Pro white-label plan costs you $297/month (as of Sept 2024). Retail GHL Pro is $199/month, but white-label is a separate tier. Custom branding, API access, and client support fall under white-label pricing.
> - **Healthy markup range**: 2x–4x is standard for SaaS white-label resale. At 2x (sell for $594/month), you capture $297/month margin per client. At 3x (sell for $891/month), margin is $594/month. At 4x (sell for $1,188/month), margin is $891/month. Choose based on market (agencies in high-cost cities support 4x; competitive markets need 2–2.5x).
> - **Best pricing sweet spot for most agencies**: Sell GHL white-label at $599–$799/month. This is 2–2.7x markup, feels reasonable to clients (less than $1,000/month), and nets $302–$502/month margin per client. At 30 clients, that's $9,060–$15,060/month gross margin.
> - **Profit after overhead**: If you charge $699/month and GHL costs you $297/month, gross margin is $402/month per client. Subtract support (estimated $50–$100/month per active client for onboarding, training, troubleshooting), and net margin is $300–$350/month. At 50 clients, that's $15,000–$17,500/month net profit (before platform hosting, payment processing, staff salaries).
> - **Competitor pricing (as of Sept 2024)**: Kajabi's white-label ($499–$999/month) is pricier than GHL but includes email, courses, and community. Pipedrive's white-label ($79–$229/month) is cheaper but has lower feature density. Custom CRM builders (Bubble, Make) cost $300–$500/month platform + 100+ hours dev, making them uncompetitive vs. GHL's prebuilt white-label. GHL white-label undercuts most competitors on price while offering more features.
> - **Pricing psychology**: Clients care less about absolute price and more about perceived value. Price at $799/month (emphasize "includes unlimited users, SMS, landing pages, calendar") rather than $297 cost + markup (clients don't care about your margin). Use annual prepay discounts (e.g., $699/month or $7,500/year, save $888) to lock in revenue and improve cash flow. Tier pricing ($299 Starter, $699 Pro, $1,299 Enterprise) allows clients to self-select and increases perceived fairness.
> - **Scaling profitably**: At 10 clients = $4,020–$5,020/month net (roughly part-time income). At 30 clients = $9,060–$15,060/month net (full-time). At 100 clients = $30,000–$50,000/month net (requires support team; hire at 50 clients). Each support hire costs ~$3,000–$4,000/month (salary + tools) but allows you to serve 50 additional clients, adding $15,000–$25,000/month revenue. ROI on support hire is 4–6 months.
> - **Churn and retention**: SaaS white-label typically has 5–8% monthly churn. At 30 clients and 5% monthly churn, you lose 1.5 clients/month (~$1,000–$1,500/month revenue). To stay at 30 clients, you must add 1.5 new clients/month. Set a target: 3 new clients/month (2x churn rate) to grow. At 4 clients/month growth, you'll reach 50 clients in ~6 months, 100 clients in ~12 months.
> - **Payment processing**: Stripe, PayPal, or Merchant services charge 2.2–3.5% + $0.30 per transaction. On $699/month client, that's ~$15–$25/month fee per client. At 50 clients, that's $750–$1,250/month (~1–2% of revenue). Offer annual prepay to reduce per-transaction fees.
> - **Do NOT underprice white-label**: At $399/month, you're charging $102/month margin per client (above GHL $297 cost). After support (~$50/month), net is $52/month (~18% net margin). You'd need 200 clients to hit $10,400/month profit. Instead, price at $699/month, get 50 clients for $20,100/month profit, and serve them better. Avoid the race to the bottom.
> - **Contract terms**: Offer month-to-month for the first 3 months (reduces adoption friction), then require annual contracts or 6-month minimums (improves retention and cash flow). Annual clients have 2–3% churn vs. 8–10% for monthly. Include clauses for price increases (5–10% annually after year 1) and feature changes (GHL updates pass through to clients; your white-label features may evolve).
> - **Support & onboarding**: Budget 4–8 hours per new client for onboarding (setup, training, documentation). At $100/hour blended labor cost, that's $400–$800 cost per client acquisition. Build this into your initial margin (charge $699/month, recover onboarding cost in first 2–3 months, then profit). Offer limited ongoing support (email, 1 quarterly call) in the base price; premium support tiers (phone, video training, strategy calls) at +$99–$299/month.

### Markup Formula

```
Markup % = ((Your Price - GHL Cost) / GHL Cost) × 100

Example:
GHL Cost = $297/month
Your Price = $699/month
Margin = $699 - $297 = $402/month
Markup = ($402 / $297) × 100 = 135% (or 2.35x)
```

## Part 4: Pricing Strategies & Psychology

### Strategy 1: Tiered Pricing (Starter / Standard / Premium)

**Model**:
```
Starter: $299/month    (GHL Starter white-label at $97; 3.1x markup)
Standard: $699/month   (GHL Pro white-label at $297; 2.35x markup)
Premium: $1,299/month  (GHL Business white-label at $597; 2.18x markup)
```

**Why tiered pricing works**:
- Allows different customer segments to self-select (reduces friction of "too expensive")
- Starter buyers often upgrade to Standard within 6–12 months (upsell revenue)
- Premium tier anchors perceived value of Standard (makes $699 feel like a deal)
- Psychological effect: 3-option choice feels more fair than 1 flat price

**Client perception**:
- Startup: "I'm not sure yet; $299 is a safe first step"
- Standard: "This is the main offering; most customers pick this"
- Premium: "For serious agencies; we get priority support"

**Expected uptake** (based on SaaS benchmarks):
- Starter: 20–30% of customers
- Standard: 50–60%
- Premium: 10–20%

**Recommended tiering by your cost structure**:

| Your Segment | Starter Price | Standard Price | Premium Price | Target Mix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Price-competitive (2x max markup)** | $199 | $499 | $799 | 25% / 60% / 15% |
| **Mid-market (2.35x markup)** | $299 | $699 | $1,199 | 25% / 55% / 20% |
| **Premium (3x+ markup)** | $399 | $999 | $1,999 | 20% / 50% / 30% |

**Pro tip**: Don't make Starter too cheap (e.g., $99). It attracts tire-kickers who churn fast, waste support time, and leave bad reviews. Minimum Starter price: $199–$299. This filters for buyers serious enough to use the tool.

### Strategy 2: Annual Prepay Discount (Lock Revenue, Improve Cash Flow)

**Model**:
```
Monthly: $699/month = $8,388/year
Annual prepay: $649/month = $7,788/year (save $600 = 7% discount)
Or: Pay once upfront: $7,500/year (save $888 = 10.6% discount)
```

**Why annual prepay works**:
- Improves cash flow (receive 12 months of payment upfront)
- Improves retention (paid upfront = higher switching cost)
- Reduces churn (annual contracts have 2–3% churn vs. 8–10% for monthly)
- Simplifies billing (one invoice/year vs. 12 invoices)

**Financial impact** (assume 30 clients, 60% convert to annual prepay):

| Scenario | Monthly Revenue | Annual Prepay (60%) | Annual Upfront Received | Cash Flow Gain | Churn Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **All monthly** | $20,970/month | — | — | Spread over 12 months | 8% churn = 2.4 clients/month lost |
| **60% annual prepay** | $20,970/month (equivalent) | $7,500 × 18 clients | $135,000 upfront | +$135,000 month 1; $8,745/month thereafter | 3% churn = 0.9 clients/month lost |

**Churn benefit**: If monthly churn is 8% and annual churn is 3%, you retain an extra 1.5 clients/month. Over a year, that's 18 clients retained = $149,400 additional revenue (18 × $699 × 12).

**Discount guidance**:
- 5–7% discount for annual prepay (standard, expected by customers)
- 10% discount for 2-year prepay (rare; locks revenue for 24 months; use for early customers)
- Monthly billing at full price (no discount; covers transactional costs)

**Recommendation**: Offer three payment options:

```
PAYMENT OPTIONS

1. Monthly: $699/month (pay as you go, cancel anytime)
2. Annual: $649/month billed once ($7,788/year, save $600)
3. Annual Upfront: $7,500 paid today ($625/month effective, save $888)
```

Expected distribution:
