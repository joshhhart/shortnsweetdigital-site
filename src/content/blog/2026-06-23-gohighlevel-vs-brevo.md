# GoHighLevel vs Brevo: Which CRM is Best for Agencies in 2026?

You're shopping for a CRM to power your agency. Two platforms keep coming up: GoHighLevel and Brevo.

GoHighLevel feels like it was built for agencies—white-label sub-accounts, unlimited contacts, deep automation. Brevo feels like email-first, recently expanding into CRM territory with an attractive price tag. Both claim to be "the best for agencies." Neither is wrong, but they're built for different problems.

This guide compares GoHighLevel and Brevo across 10 dimensions: pricing, features, automation, white-labeling, scalability, ease of use, support, integrations, and ideal use cases. By the end, you'll know exactly which one fits your agency.

> **Key Takeaways**
> - GoHighLevel ($199–$497/month) is purpose-built for white-label agencies with unlimited contacts and deep automation; Brevo ($20–$300/month) is email-first and cheaper but requires workarounds for multi-client agency setups
> - GoHighLevel's sub-accounts allow you to manage 50+ clients from one dashboard; Brevo's workspace model is less isolated and requires more manual setup per client
> - Brevo wins on email marketing features (templates, deliverability, compliance) and entry price ($20/month); GoHighLevel wins on sales automation and white-label professionalism
> - GoHighLevel charges $199–$497/month per sub-account (scalable cost); Brevo charges by contact volume, so high-volume agencies pay similarly to GoHighLevel but with less feature depth
> - For agencies managing 5+ clients or needing white-label branding, GoHighLevel is the stronger choice; for email-focused freelancers or solo consultants, Brevo is cheaper and simpler

## Pricing: The Headline Numbers

This is often the first question. Pricing shapes everything else.

### GoHighLevel Pricing

| Plan | Monthly | Contacts | Sub-Accounts | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | $99 | Up to 2,500 | ❌ None | Solo practitioners (no white-label) |
| **Pro** | $199 | Up to 10,000 | ✅ Unlimited | Agencies (1–20 clients) |
| **Unlimited** | $497 | Unlimited | ✅ Unlimited | High-volume agencies (20+ clients) |

**Total cost example**: You're managing 5 clients, each on Pro tier.
```
Parent account: $199 (Pro)
Client 1 sub-account: $199
Client 2 sub-account: $199
Client 3 sub-account: $199
Client 4 sub-account: $199
Client 5 sub-account: $199
─────────────────────────────
Total: $1,194/month
```

**Margin example** (if you charge clients $399/month each):
```
Revenue: 5 clients × $399 = $1,995/month
Cost: 5 × $199 = $995/month
Profit: $1,000/month (50% margin)
```

### Brevo Pricing

Brevo uses a contact-based model (simpler upfront, potentially expensive at scale).

| Contacts | Monthly Cost | Email Limit | CRM Features | SMS Included |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Up to 500 | Free (forever) | Unlimited | Limited | No |
| 500–5,000 | $20 | Unlimited | CRM + Automations | No |
| 5,000–50,000 | $300 | Unlimited | CRM + Automations + API | Yes |
| 50,000+ | Custom quote | Unlimited | Enterprise features | Yes |

**Total cost example**: Same 5 clients, each with 2,000 contacts (10,000 total).
```
Brevo tier for 10,000 contacts: $300/month
Cost per client: $300 ÷ 5 = $60/month (illusory—it's one bill)
```

Wait—this seems cheap. But there's a catch: **Brevo charges by total contact volume across all clients, not per-client.** If you're managing 50,000 contacts across 5 clients, you pay one bill for 50,000 contacts. This works fine until:

1. You add a 6th client with 10,000 contacts (now 60,000 total; bill jumps)
2. You want to isolate billing per client (can't—Brevo doesn't support per-client billing)
3. A client wants data separation (Brevo's workspaces are soft; clients could theoretically access other client data if given the URL)

### Pricing Comparison: The Reality

| Scenario | GoHighLevel | Brevo |
|---|---|---|
| **1 solo consultant, 1,000 contacts** | $99/month (Starter) | Free (if ≤500) or $20/month |
| **1 agency, 5 clients × 2,000 contacts each** | $1,194/month (1 parent + 5 Pro subs) | $300/month (one invoice for 10K contacts) |
| **1 agency, 10 clients × 5,000 contacts each** | $2,391/month (1 parent Pro + 10 Pro subs) or $4,970/month (all Unlimited) | Custom pricing (50K+ contacts; likely $500–$1,000/month) |

**Brevo is cheaper at first.** But as you scale (more clients, more contacts), GoHighLevel's per-sub-account model becomes more predictable and easier to margin on.

## Migration: Moving from Brevo to GoHighLevel

If you start with Brevo and outgrow it, here's how to migrate:

### Step 1: Export Data from Brevo

1. Go to **CRM** → **Contacts**
2. Click **Export** (or **Bulk Actions** → **Export**)
3. Select all contacts
4. Download as CSV
5. Save file (e.g., `brevo-contacts-2026-06-23.csv`)

### Step 2: Create a GoHighLevel Sub-Account

1. Log into your GoHighLevel parent account
2. Go to **Settings** → **Accounts** → **+ Create Sub-Account**
3. Enter client details (name, email, tier)
4. Finish setup (white-label branding, permissions)

### Step 3: Import Contacts to GoHighLevel

1. In the GoHighLevel sub-account, go to **Contacts** → **Import**
2. Click **Upload CSV**
3. Map columns (first name, last name, email, etc.) to match your CSV
4. Review preview
5. Click **Import**

GoHighLevel deduplicates and imports contacts (usually takes 5–30 minutes).

### Step 4: Rebuild Workflows

Brevo workflows don't export. You'll need to manually recreate them in GoHighLevel.

**Time estimate**: 2–4 hours for 5–10 workflows (not as bad as it sounds).

### Step 5: Update Login Details

Send your client their new GoHighLevel login (custom domain, if set up):

> "Your CRM has moved to a new platform with even more features. Log in here: https://crm.youragency.com
> Email: [their email]
> Password: [temporary, sent separately]"

## FAQ: GoHighLevel vs Brevo

### Q: Can I use both (Brevo for email, GoHighLevel for CRM)?

**A**: Yes. You can set up Zapier to sync Brevo contacts to GoHighLevel. But this adds complexity. Pick one platform and stick with it.

---

### Q: Does Brevo offer white-label sub-accounts like GoHighLevel?

**A**: No. Brevo's workspace model is shared. You can customize colors and add your logo, but clients still see "Powered by Brevo" and the login is always brevo.com. Not true white-label.

---

### Q: What if I need both email marketing (Brevo-strength) and CRM (GoHighLevel-strength)?

**A**: Use GoHighLevel for everything. Its email features are solid (4/5 stars), and you avoid managing two tools. The extra cost of GoHighLevel is worth the unified experience.

---

### Q: Can I switch from GoHighLevel to Brevo later?

**A**: Yes, but it's tedious. Export contacts as CSV, import to Brevo. Rebuilding workflows takes time. It's easier to go Brevo → GoHighLevel (as described above) than the reverse.

---

### Q: Does GoHighLevel offer a free trial?

**A**: Yes, 14-day free trial (credit card required). Brevo offers a free plan forever (up to 500 contacts + limited features).

---

### Q: What's the learning curve for someone non-technical?

**A**: Brevo: 1–2 weeks. GoHighLevel: 2–4 weeks. Both are learnable by non-technical users. GoHighLevel has more options, so more upfront confusion.

---

### Q: If I pick GoHighLevel, do I need to use it for my own business too?

**A**: Not required, but recommended. Use it to manage your own leads, clients, and sales pipeline. You'll understand the platform better and can support clients more effectively.
