# GoHighLevel vs AppointmentCore: Features, Pricing & Best Fit

You're running a coaching practice or digital agency. You need a platform that handles scheduling, client relationships, and automations—all in one place. Two popular options keep appearing in your research: **GoHighLevel** and **AppointmentCore**.

Both promise to replace multiple tools with one integrated suite. But they solve different problems, target different users, and cost differently. The question: Which is right for your business?

This comparison covers pricing, core features, automation depth, integrations, customer support, and real-world fit for three scenarios: solo coaches, small agencies, and scaling practices.

> **Key Takeaways**
> - **GoHighLevel**: Full CRM + automation + white-label + 50+ integrations ($199–$299/month); best for agencies scaling clients
> - **AppointmentCore**: Scheduling + basic CRM + lite automations ($49–$149/month); best for solo/small coaches wanting simplicity
> - **Pricing difference**: AppointmentCore costs $150/month less at entry level, but lacks white-label and advanced workflows
> - **Automation scope**: GoHighLevel supports complex multi-step automations; AppointmentCore supports basic trigger-based workflows
> - **Integration ecosystem**: GoHighLevel native integrations (Stripe, Zapier, Facebook Ads, YouTube); AppointmentCore relies on Zapier for most
> - **Customer support**: GoHighLevel has 24/7 chat and phone; AppointmentCore has email and knowledge base (no phone support)
> - **Use case alignment**: Choose GoHighLevel if you resell to clients or manage 20+ client relationships; choose AppointmentCore if you're a solo service provider prioritizing scheduling
> - **Data export**: Both allow CSV export; GoHighLevel includes API access for developers; AppointmentCore API is limited (read-only for most endpoints)
> - **Learning curve**: AppointmentCore is easier for non-technical users (dashboard is simpler); GoHighLevel has steeper learning curve but more power
> - **Trial access**: GoHighLevel offers 14-day free trial; AppointmentCore offers 30-day free trial

## Phase 3: Automation Depth: Real-World Comparison

### Scenario A: Welcome Sequence for New Clients (Solo Coach)

**The workflow**: New client books first appointment → Auto-send welcome email → 24 hours before appointment, send SMS reminder → After appointment, send feedback form.

**GoHighLevel automation** (possible—multi-step):
1. Trigger: **Appointment Created**
2. Step 1: **Send Email** (welcome, personalized)
3. Step 2: **Wait 24 Hours**
4. Step 3: **Check Condition**: Is appointment time within 24 hours?
   - If yes, send SMS
   - If no, skip
5. Step 4: **Wait Until Appointment Completion** (via webhook or manual trigger)
6. Step 6: **Send Form** (feedback survey)
7. Step 7: **Tag Contact** (as "Completed First Session")

**Result**: Fully automated, no manual touches. Estimated setup: 10 minutes.

**AppointmentCore automation** (limited—single-trigger):
1. **Booking Confirmation**: Auto-send templated email (built-in)
2. **Appointment Reminder**: Auto-send SMS 24 hours before (built-in)
3. **Post-Appointment Feedback**: Not supported (use Zapier workaround)
   - Workaround: Create Zapier zap that watches AppointmentCore for "Appointment Completed" → Send email via third-party

**Result**: Partially automated; requires Zapier for feedback flow. Estimated setup: 15 minutes (including Zapier).

**Verdict**: GoHighLevel handles this natively and faster. AppointmentCore requires Zapier workaround for step 3.

---

### Scenario B: Lead Qualification Workflow (Small Agency)

**The workflow**: Lead fills form → If budget > $5,000, tag as "Hot Lead" and send to strategist immediately; if budget < $1,000, tag as "Low Priority" and add to nurture sequence.

**GoHighLevel automation** (fully supported):
1. Trigger: **Form Submitted**
2. Step 1: **Create Contact**
3. Step 2: **Check Condition**: Custom Field "Budget" > 5000?
   - If yes: Tag = "Hot Lead", Send SMS to strategist, Add to "Hot Lead Nurture" sequence
   - If no: Tag = "Low Priority", Add to "General Nurture" sequence

**Result**: Completely automated, multi-branch logic. Estimated setup: 5 minutes.

**AppointmentCore automation** (not supported):
- AppointmentCore has no form trigger for this scenario (no form builder)
- Workaround: Use Zapier to watch form submission → Conditionally create AppointmentCore contact with tag
- Still requires manual tag assignment based on budget (no conditional tags in AppointmentCore)

**Verdict**: GoHighLevel natively; AppointmentCore requires complex Zapier workaround and still won't auto-tag based on budget.

---

### Scenario C: Upsell Trigger (Coaching Practice)

**The workflow**: Client completes 5 coaching sessions → Auto-send offer for 1-on-1 intensive program ($2,000 upsell).

**GoHighLevel automation** (fully supported):
1. Trigger: **Contact Completes Sequence** (track appointment count via automation steps)
2. Step 1: **Check Condition**: Appointment count = 5?
   - If yes: Send upsell email, create task for coach, tag as "Upsell Ready"
   - If no: Do nothing

**Result**: Tracks progress automatically; fully automated upsell. Setup: 10 minutes.

**AppointmentCore automation** (not supported):
- No automation trigger for "5 appointments completed"
- Workaround: Use manual report (view client appointment history) and manually tag/send upsell email
- Or: Zapier zap that counts AppointmentCore appointments and triggers email after 5

**Verdict**: GoHighLevel is automatic and reliable. AppointmentCore is manual or requires complex Zapier logic.
