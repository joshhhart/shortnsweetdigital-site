# GoHighLevel Pricing Plans & Costs: Starter vs. Pro vs. Unlimited (2026)

You're comparing CRM platforms. GoHighLevel keeps coming up. But the pricing question stops you: Is it worth $99–$497/month?

By the end, you'll know exactly which plan fits your budget and growth stage.

> - White-label SaaS mode (Unlimited plan only) lets you resell GoHighLevel to your clients at a 40–50% commission, creating recurring revenue ($10K–$50K/year for a 10–50 client base)

## Plan Deep Dive: What Each Tier Includes

### Starter Plan ($99/Month)

**Best for**: Solo entrepreneurs, freelancers, small consultants with 10–50 active leads.

**What you get**:
- 1 user account (you only)
- Unlimited contacts, email campaigns, automations
- 500 SMS/month
- 500 calls/month
- 5 landing pages
- Basic CRM and pipeline management
- Form builder
- Task management
- Limited API (webhooks, Zapier integration)

**Constraints**:
- No team members—you can't add assistants or collaborators
- SMS is throttled (500/month = ~12 messages/day for a 40-day month)
- Calls limited (500/month = ~12 calls/day)
- No white-label option
- Limited integrations (Zapier available, but not native Microsoft Teams or Slack)

**Cost breakdown**:
- Plan: $99/month
- SMS overages: If you exceed 500, pay $0.01–$0.02 per SMS (~$50 for 5K extra SMS)
- **Total typical monthly cost: $99–$124**

**Real-world scenario: Freelance consultant**

You're a freelance marketing consultant. You get 20–30 qualified leads/month from your website and referrals. Starter is enough because:
- You manage everything yourself
- You don't have team coordination overhead
- 500 SMS/month covers weekly check-ins with 10–15 active leads
- You're not white-labeling

**Pros**:
- Cheapest entry point ($1,200/year)
- All core CRM features (email, SMS, automation, forms)
- Works great for solopreneurs

**Cons**:
- Can't hire a VA (no extra user seat)
- SMS limits hit if you scale to 50+ leads
- No white-label resale option

---

### Pro Plan ($199/Month)

**Best for**: Freelancers scaling to teams, agencies with 2–3 team members, small businesses managing 50–200 active leads.

**What you get**:
- 3 team seats (you + 2 collaborators/admins)
- Unlimited contacts, email campaigns, automations, landing pages
- 5,000 SMS/month
- 2,000 calls/month
- Full CRM and pipeline management
- Advanced automation builder
- Reporting and analytics
- Full API access
- Zapier, Make (Integromat), Slack, Microsoft Teams, and 500+ other integrations

**Constraints**:
- Max 3 team members (if you have 4+ agents/employees, you need Unlimited)
- No white-label option (you can't rebrand GoHighLevel for clients)
- Calls still metered (2,000/month = 67/day average)

**Cost breakdown**:
- Plan: $199/month
- SMS included: 5,000/month (covers 150+ lead touchpoints, 5x/month per lead)
- Extra SMS overage: If you exceed 5K, ~$50 for 5K more
- **Total typical monthly cost: $199–$249**

**Real-world scenario: Agency with 3-person team**

You run a small marketing agency. You have 2 account managers and a scheduler/admin. You're managing 200 leads across 10–15 client campaigns. Pro is the right fit because:
- 3 team seats = you + 2 managers + 1 scheduler (rotate or limit to 3)
- 5,000 SMS/month covers 100–150 active leads touched 3–5x/month
- Full API access lets you build custom integrations
- Reporting shows performance by team member and campaign

**Pros**:
- 10x more SMS than Starter
- 3 team seats (scale from solo to small team)
- Full API + integrations
- Affordable for agencies ($199 cost vs. $500–$1,000/month for dedicated CRM tools like HubSpot)

**Cons**:
- Limited to 3 team members (4th person = Unlimited upgrade)
- No white-label option
- Calls metered (2,000/month is low for call-heavy sales teams)

## Hidden Costs: What's NOT Included in Base Pricing

GoHighLevel's base price is clean, but there are optional add-ons and overages to budget for:

### 1. SMS Overages

| Plan | Included SMS | Overage Cost per 5K SMS |
|------|--------------|------------------------|
| Starter | 500/month | $25–$50 |
| Pro & Unlimited | 5,000/month | $25–$50 |

If you exceed your monthly SMS allowance, you pay extra. Example:
- Pro plan: 5,000 SMS included
- You send 7,500 SMS = 2,500 overage
- Cost: $25 (for 2,500) = $199 + $25 = **$224 total that month**

**Budget rule**: If you're managing 100+ leads, budget an extra $50–$100/month for SMS overages (especially if you're in sales, real estate, or healthcare—industries that lean heavily on SMS).

---

### 2. Call Credits (Unlimited Plan)

Unlimited plan includes unlimited call minutes, but you prepay for call credits. Typical cost: $0.05–$0.15 per call minute.

Example:
- 50 calls/month × 10 min average = 500 call minutes
- 500 × $0.10 = $50/month in call credits
- **Total Unlimited cost: $497 + $50 = $547/month**

**Budget rule**: If you're using VoIP heavily (50+ calls/month), add $50–$150/month for call credits.

---

### 3. Integrations & Add-Ons

Most integrations are free (Zapier, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Calendar). But some third-party services charge:

| Integration | Cost |
|-------------|------|
| **Zapier** (Unlimited tasks) | $20–$200/month (depending on task volume) |
| **Stripe** (Payment processing) | 2.2% + $0.30 per transaction (GoHighLevel takes small cut) |
| **Twilio SMS** (Custom SMS sender ID) | $0–$20/month |
| **Calendar (Outlook/Google)** | Free |
| **Slack** | Free |
| **Custom API integration** | $0 (but may require developer time: $1K–$5K one-time) |

**Budget rule**: Most teams don't need paid integrations. Budget $0–$50/month unless you're heavy on Zapier automations.

---

### 4. Training, Onboarding & Setup

GoHighLevel has a learning curve. Many teams budget for onboarding help:

| Service | Cost |
|---------|------|
| **GoHighLevel official onboarding** | $0–$500 (free for Unlimited; paid consulting for others) |
| **Certified coach/implementation partner** | $500–$2,500 one-time |
| **Custom automation build** | $1,000–$5,000 (agency partner develops complex workflows) |
| **Staff training** | $0 (DIY via YouTube) or $500–$1,500 (live training sessions) |

**Budget rule**: First-year cost often includes $500–$1,500 for onboarding. After year 1, it's just the monthly subscription.

**Bottom line**: Budget 2–3x the base monthly cost for Year 1 (setup, training, overages). Year 2+ is just the base cost plus minor overages.

## Honest Truth: When GoHighLevel Pricing Doesn't Make Sense

GoHighLevel isn't the cheapest CRM. It's the most affordable *all-in-one*. When it doesn't make sense:

| Scenario | Better Alternative |

GoHighLevel shines for: **Sales-heavy businesses managing lead pipelines with email, SMS, and team coordination.** If you fit that profile, it's the best value.
