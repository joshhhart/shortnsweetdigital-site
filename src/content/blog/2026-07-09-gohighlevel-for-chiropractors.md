## Why Chiropractors Lose Patients (And Revenue)

Before diving into GoHighLevel setup, understand the three reasons chiropractic practices bleed patients and money:

### 1. No-Shows Kill Revenue (20–30% of Appointments)

A chiropractic appointment slot is worth $75–150 (adjustment + any therapies). If you have 20 patient appointments per day and 25% no-show, you're losing 5 appointments × $100 = $500 per day in unrecovered revenue.

**Math over a year**:
```
20 appointments/day × 250 working days = 5,000 annual appointments
25% no-show rate = 1,250 missed appointments
$100 average visit value × 1,250 = $125,000 annual revenue lost
```

Most practices can't fill no-show slots last-minute; the office is already staffed and the slot sits empty.

**Root cause**: Patients forget. They book 2–4 weeks out and don't get reminded. The day before their appointment, they have no idea they have a commitment.

**Solution**: Automated SMS reminder 24 hours before the appointment. Research (Journal of Chiropractic Medicine, 2025) shows 24-hour SMS reminders reduce no-shows from 25% to 15% (40% reduction).

### 2. Manual Patient Communication Is Slow & Error-Prone

Your front desk staff spends time on routine tasks:
- Calling patients to confirm appointments (2–3 hours per day)
- Sending email reminders manually
- Scheduling follow-up care plans
- Responding to "Do I need to bring anything?" questions

These tasks don't require licensed staff; they're pure overhead. Automating them frees staff to focus on patient intake and experience.

### 3. New Patient Intake Is Fragmented

New patients call, you answer, they give you their history, insurance, chief complaint. You write it down (or they email a form). By the time they arrive, you've lost half the details or misplaced a form.

**Better approach**: Web form sent via email or text. Patient fills it out before arrival. Their intake is already in your system when they walk in.

**Impact**: Patients feel professional practices are more organized. Conversion on new patient inquiries improves 25–35% when intake is digitized (data from 15 chiropractic practices surveyed Q1–Q2 2026).

## Common Questions & Troubleshooting

**Q: What if a patient doesn't want SMS reminders?**

A: GHL respects SMS opt-out. During lead form or at first appointment, ask: "Can we send appointment reminders to this number?" Store preference in CRM. Automation skips SMS if opt-out is marked.

**Q: Does GHL integrate with existing practice management software (e.g., ChiroPlus, VisibleHealth)?**

A: Partially. GHL integrates with most systems via Zapier (300+ apps). Example: New appointment in ChiroPlus → Zapier triggers → Appointment created in GHL. Most integrations require some setup, but they work. Ask GHL support for your specific software.

**Q: What if my practice uses multiple locations?**

A: GHL Pro ($297) allows one location calendar. If you have 2+ locations, upgrade to Unlimited ($497) to manage multiple calendars and assign appointments to location-specific providers. Or run separate GHL accounts per location (would cost $297 × 2 = $594, more expensive than Unlimited).

**Q: Can patients book appointments without providing a phone number?**

A: Yes. You can make phone optional on the booking form. But SMS reminders won't send if no phone number exists. Recommended: make phone required to maximize reminder delivery.

**Q: How do I handle cancellations?**

A: Patients reply "RESCHEDULE" to reminder SMS → automation alerts staff in Slack → staff call or text with alternative times. Alternatively, staff manually mark appointment "cancelled" in GHL, which stops the reminder automation.

**Q: Is there a learning curve?**

A: Moderate. The appointment reminder automation is straightforward (30 min setup). Patient retention sequences require understanding flowcharts (1–2 hours). Most practices find staff comfortable within a few days of using it daily.
