By the end, you'll have a fully automated sales engine that turns prospects into customers 24/7.

## Step 1: Create Your Funnel and Configure Basic Settings

![Step 1: Create Your Funnel and Configure Basic Settings](/images/2026-08-04-gohighlevel-funnel-builder-step-by-step-guide-s2.jpg)


### 1a. Create a New Funnel

1. Log into GoHighLevel (app.gohighlevel.com)
2. Go to **Sidebar → Funnels** (or **Funnel Builder**)
3. Click **+ New Funnel** or **Create Funnel**
4. You'll see a funnel template selection screen with options:
   - **Webinar Funnel**: Lead capture → Email sequence → Live/recorded webinar → Sales page → Payment
   - **Sales Funnel**: Lead capture → Email sequence → Sales page → Payment
   - **Service Funnel**: Lead capture → Application form → Calendar booking → Payment
   - **Lead Generation**: Lead capture → Lead scoring → Sales team assignment
   - **Blank Funnel**: Start from scratch (advanced users)

5. Choose **Sales Funnel** (most common; works for consulting, courses, digital products, services)
6. Click **Create Funnel**
7. Name your funnel:
   - Name: "Service Name - Sales Funnel 2026" (e.g., "Web Design Package - Sales Funnel 2026")
   - Description (optional): "Convert website design leads into paying clients"
   - Click **Save**

### 1b. Understand Funnel Structure

The funnel template comes with pre-built pages in a sequence:

```
Landing Page (Opt-in) → Thank You / Email Sequence Page → 
Sales Page → Payment Page → Confirmation Page
```

**What happens**:
1. Prospect lands on your landing page
2. Enters email and clicks CTA (e.g., "Get Free Consultation")
3. Auto-added to email sequence (welcome email triggered)
4. Receives follow-up emails (Days 1, 3, 5, 7)
5. Email contains link to sales page (product/service description)
6. Prospect clicks "Buy Now" on sales page
7. Lands on payment page
8. Enters payment details and completes purchase
9. Receives confirmation page + confirmation email

**You'll customize each of these pages in Steps 2–5.**

### 1c. Configure Funnel Domain Settings

By default, funnel is hosted on GHL's free domain (e.g., `yourname.gohighlevel.io`). You can use this or point a custom domain.

**Option 1: Use Free GHL Domain** (Recommended for beginners)
- No DNS setup required
- Ready to use immediately
- Domain looks: `yourname.gohighlevel.io/sales-funnel`
- Skip to Step 2a

**Option 2: Use Custom Domain** (Recommended for professionals)
1. In funnel settings, go to **Domain**
2. Click **Add Custom Domain**
3. Enter your domain (e.g., `sales.yourname.com`)
4. GHL will show DNS records you need to add:
   - Typically: CNAME record pointing to GHL servers
   - Example: `sales.yourname.com CNAME yourname.gohighlevel.com`
5. Log into your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.)
6. Add the CNAME record to DNS
7. Wait 24–48 hours for DNS to propagate
8. Return to GHL → Verify domain
9. Once verified, your funnel is live at `sales.yourname.com`

**By end of Step 1, you should have**:
- ✅ Funnel created and named
- ✅ Domain configured (GHL free or custom)
- ✅ Funnel structure understood (5 pages)

## Step 2: Build Your Landing Page

### 2a. Edit the Landing Page

The funnel template includes a default landing page. You'll customize it for your offer.

1. In funnel builder, click **Landing Page** (first page in the sequence)
2. You'll see a visual editor showing:
   - Header section (logo, navigation)
   - Hero section (main image + headline + CTA button)
   - Body sections (features, benefits, testimonials)
   - Footer

3. Edit each section:

**Header**:
- Logo (upload yours or use text)
- Navigation (optional; usually hidden on landing pages)

**Hero Section** (most important):
- **Headline**: Your main promise or question
  - Good examples: "Close More Deals in 30 Days", "Free Website Audit", "Save 10 Hours Per Week"
  - Bad examples: "Welcome", "Check This Out", "Important"
  - **Rule**: Front-load benefit; speak to prospect's problem or goal
- **Subheading**: One sentence explaining what they'll get
  - Good examples: "Learn the exact system we use to sign 10+ clients/month", "No credit card required"
  - Bad examples: "This is great", "You'll love this"
- **Hero Image**: High-quality image or video related to offer
  - Recommended: Screenshot of your product, before/after image, team photo, or lifestyle image
  - Avoid: Stock photos that look generic or unrelated
- **CTA Button**: Clear, action-oriented text
  - Good examples: "Get Free Audit", "Claim Your Spot", "Buy Now", "Schedule Demo"
  - Button color: Contrast with background (blue, green, orange typically work)
  - Button size: Large enough to tap on mobile

**Body Sections** (3–5 sections):
1. **Section 1 - The Problem**: "You're wasting hours on manual follow-up"
   - Describe prospect's pain point (not your solution yet)
   - Use your research and field data to validate the problem
   - Include statistic if relevant
2. **Section 2 - The Solution**: "Automated sales funnels fix this"
   - Describe your solution/offer
   - Keep it benefit-focused (not feature-focused)
   - Include image/icon
3. **Section 3 - Key Results/Outcomes**: "Clients get X, Y, Z"
   - List 3–5 outcomes prospect will achieve
   - Use bullet points
   - Include data if you have it ("Save 10 hours/week", "Close 3x more deals")
4. **Section 4 - Testimonials/Social Proof** (optional but recommended):
   - 2–3 customer quotes or case results
   - Include name, title, company
   - Link to case study if available
5. **Section 5 - FAQ or Objection Handler**:
   - Answer 3–5 common questions
   - Address prospect concerns before they bounce
   - Example: "How long does this take to set up?" "Do I need technical skills?"

**Footer**:
- Privacy policy link (required)
- Terms of service link (recommended)
- Contact email
- Copyright notice (auto-populated)

### 2b. Optimize for Conversions

Once copy is in place, apply these conversion best practices:

1. **CTA Button Placement**:
   - Hero CTA: In hero section (above the fold)
   - Mid-page CTA: After section 2 or 3 (after you've built interest)
   - Bottom CTA: At the very bottom (for readers who scrolled all the way)
   - **Rule**: 2–3 CTAs max per page (too many dilutes focus)

2. **Form Fields**:
   - Landing page should include a form (email capture at minimum)
   - Recommended fields:
     - Email (required)
     - First Name (required)
     - Phone (optional, increases friction; only add if needed for followup)
   - **Rule**: Fewer fields = higher conversion (more than 3 fields tanks signups)

3. **Copy Tone**:
   - Match your brand voice (professional, casual, authoritative, etc.)
   - Use "you/your" language (not "we/our")
   - Speak to the prospect's goal or problem (not features)
   - Use short sentences and paragraphs
   - Avoid jargon unless audience is technical

4. **Mobile Optimization**:
   - Preview page on mobile (GHL editor shows mobile view)
   - Ensure:
     - CTA button is easy to tap (larger on mobile)
     - Text is readable (18px+ font size)
     - Images scale correctly
     - No horizontal scrolling required
   - GHL is mobile-responsive by default; verify it looks good

5. **Visual Elements**:
   - Use 1–2 colors throughout (brand colors or web-safe contrasts)
   - Use whitespace (don't cram content)
   - Images should be high-quality and relevant
   - Icons can break up text sections

### 2c. Test Landing Page

Before moving to Step 3, test your landing page:

1. **Preview page**: Click **Preview** in the editor
2. **Fill out the form**: Test the form submission
   - Enter test email: test@example.com
   - Enter first name: Test
   - Click CTA button
   - You should see a confirmation page and receive a test email
   - **Action**: Verify the thank you page loads correctly

3. **Mobile test**: 
   - Use phone to visit the landing page URL
   - Ensure it's responsive and readable
   - Try the form on mobile

4. **Copy review**:
   - Read headline aloud; does it resonate?
   - Are the benefits clear in 30 seconds?
   - Is the CTA obvious (do you know what happens when you click)?

5. **If any issues**:
   - Missing CTA or form: Edit the page and add it
   - Copy doesn't match your tone: Rewrite sections
   - Mobile looks broken: GHL editor has mobile view; adjust layout
   - Form not submitting: Check form settings; ensure fields are configured

**By end of Step 2, you should have**:
- ✅ Landing page customized with your offer
- ✅ Headline, copy, and CTA written
- ✅ Form configured (email, first name minimum)
- ✅ Landing page tested on desktop and mobile

## Step 3: Set Up Email Automation Sequence

### 3a. Create the Welcome Email

When a prospect signs up on your landing page, they immediately receive a welcome email. Set this up first:

1. In funnel builder, go to **Automations** or **Email Sequence**
2. Find the **Welcome Email** (first email in the sequence)
3. Edit the email:
   - **Subject line**: First email the prospect sees
     - Good examples: "Your [Resource] is ready", "Welcome to [Program Name]", "Next Steps"
     - Bad examples: "Email 1", "Check This Out"
   - **Sender name**: Your name or company
   - **Sender email**: Your email address (info@domain.com or your personal email)
   - **Body**:
     ```
     Hi [FirstName],

     Thanks for signing up for [your offer]. I'm excited to help you [main benefit].

     Here's what happens next:
     - Today: You get [resource] (attached or linked below)
     - Day 3: I'll send you a case study showing results
     - Day 5: I'll ask if you're ready to move forward

     Click below to download your [resource]:
     [Link or Button]

     Questions? Reply to this email and I'll get back to you within 24 hours.

     [Your Name]
     ```
   - **Key elements**:
     - Personalization: Use [FirstName] merge tag (GHL auto-fills)
     - Clear value: What are they getting right now?
     - Set expectations: Mention the follow-up sequence
     - CTA: Link or button to next step (download resource, watch video, etc.)

4. Test the email:
   - Send test email to yourself
   - Verify merge tags work (name appears correctly)
   - Verify links work
   - Verify email looks good in Outlook, Gmail, and mobile

### 3b. Create Follow-Up Emails (Days 3, 5, 7, 10)

After the welcome email, automate a sequence of follow-ups:

**Email 2 (Day 3): Value-Add Email**
- Topic: Share a case study, article, or tip related to your offer
- Purpose: Build credibility and keep prospect engaged
- CTA: Soft CTA (e.g., "Let me know if you have questions")
- Example:
  ```
  Subject: One client's result (case study)
  
  Hi [FirstName],

  I wanted to share a quick case study from one of my recent clients:

  [Brief story: Their situation, what they implemented, the result]
  
  Result: [Metric, e.g., "Closed 8 deals in 30 days, up from 2–3/month"]

  This is typical of what I see when someone implements this system correctly.

  Questions? Reply to this email.

  [Your Name]
  ```

**Email 3 (Day 5): Objection-Handler Email**
- Topic: Address a common objection or concern
- Purpose: Remove barriers to purchase
- CTA: Soft CTA (e.g., "Want to see if this fits your situation?")
- Common objections to address:
  - "I don't have time to implement this"
  - "What if I don't have the technical skills?"
  - "How much does this cost?"
  - "Will this work for my industry?"
- Example:
  ```
  Subject: "Will this work for me?"

  Hi [FirstName],

  I often get asked: "Will this system work if I don't have a tech background?"

  Answer: Yes. In fact, most of my clients are non-technical. Here's why:

  [Explain why non-technical folks can succeed]
  [Reference a case study or testimonial]

  The technical stuff is handled by [system/tool/you]. You just follow the steps.

  Want to explore if this is right for your situation?
  [Link to consultation or sales page]

  [Your Name]
  ```

**Email 4 (Day 7): Benefit-Reinforcement Email**
- Topic: Revisit the main benefit with a different angle
- Purpose: Remind prospect why they signed up
- CTA: Soft to medium CTA (e.g., "Let's talk about your goals")
- Example:
  ```
  Subject: The real cost of not doing this

  Hi [FirstName],

  I was thinking about our conversation...

  Right now, you're spending [X hours] per week on [problem task]. 
  In a year, that's [X hours × 50 weeks] = [large number] hours.

  At [your hourly rate], that's [$ value] wasted per year.

  My system cuts that time down to [X hours] per week, saving you [$ value] annually.

  Ready to talk about implementation?
  [Link to consultation or sales page]

  [Your Name]
  ```

**Email 5 (Day 10): Final Offer Email**
- Topic: Clear, direct offer with a sense of urgency
- Purpose: Convert interested prospects before they forget
- CTA: Strong CTA (e.g., "Buy Now", "Schedule Your Consultation")
- Example:
  ```
  Subject: Last chance to lock in this price

  Hi [FirstName],

  This is my last email about [your offer].

  If you've found value in the case studies and examples I've shared, and you're serious about [goal], 
  let's work together.

  Here's the deal:
  - [Offer/package name]
  - [3–5 key deliverables]
  - Price: [Price]
  - Timeline: [Timeline, e.g., "Results in 30 days"]

  Spots are limited to [number] clients per month. I'm down to [remaining] available slots.

  Ready?
  [Link to payment or sales page]

  [Your Name]
  ```
