# GoHighLevel Unlimited Plan: True Cost Breakdown

You're evaluating GoHighLevel's **Unlimited plan** for your agency or local business. The advertised price is **$120/month**, but when you add SMS, payment processing, phone numbers, and extras, the real cost climbs fast.

This guide calculates your **true monthly spend**, shows you where hidden costs hide, and walks you through a scenario-based ROI model so you know exactly what GHL will cost *your* operation before you commit.

> **Key Takeaways**
> - **Base Unlimited subscription**: $120/month (unlimited contacts, email, landing pages, automation).
> - **SMS add-on**: $30–$50/month (for SMS automations; does not include per-SMS message costs, which vary 0.5–3¢ per SMS depending on carrier).
> - **Payment processing**: 2.2% + $0.30 per transaction (Stripe/PayPal; rates verified October 2026). On $100K annual revenue, add ~$2,500–$3,500/year.
> - **Dedicated phone number**: $29/month (for inbound calls, SMS sender ID, or voice automations).
> - **Phone (Pay-Per-Minute)**: $0.09–$0.15/minute; typical inbound call costs $0.50–$2.00/call (varies by carrier).
> - **Typical Unlimited spend for agencies**: $120 (base) + $40 (SMS, mid-range) + $29 (dedicated phone) + ~$200 (payment processing on $50K/month revenue) = **~$389/month** or **$4,668/year**.
> - **Common hidden costs**: Unused SMS credits not rolling over; overage charges if you exceed contact limits on legacy plans (Unlimited has no ceiling); Stripe/PayPal fees compounding if you run multiple revenue streams (courses, subscriptions, products).
> - **What you get at Unlimited**: Unlimited contacts, sub-accounts (white-label), full API, custom domains, advanced automations, CRM, landing pages, forms, email, SMS (if add-on purchased), two-way messaging, calendar booking.
> - **How to calculate your actual cost**: (1) Start with $120/month base. (2) Add SMS ($30–$50) if you send >1,500 SMS/month. (3) Multiply revenue by 2.2% + $0.30 per transaction. (4) Add $29 if you need inbound calls or verified SMS sender. (5) Add phone minutes ($0.09–$0.15/min) if running voice automations. (6) Total = your monthly GHL spend.
> - **When Unlimited is worth it**: You have 1,000+ contacts, run multiple automations, resell GHL (white-label), or process $20K+/month in revenue. Smaller operations should start on Starter ($40) or Professional ($70).
> - **Comparison snapshot (October 2026)**: Brevo email-only ~$60–$300/month (cheaper for email alone; no CRM or landing pages). HubSpot Starter ~$50 (basic CRM, no SMS or phone). Unlimited bundles everything in one, saving switching costs but adding complexity if you don't use all features.

## Part 2: SMS Add-On Cost

### The SMS Confusion

GHL pricing for SMS is **not** included in the Unlimited plan. You must purchase the **SMS add-on** separately.

### SMS Add-On: $30–$50/Month

The SMS add-on gives you **unlimited SMS automations** (trigger-based SMS workflows). However, you still pay for **each SMS message sent**.

#### 2.1 SMS Subscription Add-On

| Tier | Price | Monthly SMS Credits | Cost Per SMS (Overage) | Best For |
|------|-------|--------------------|-----------------------|----------|
| **SMS Starter** | $30/month | ~5,000 SMS | $0.01–$0.015 each (after credits used) | <5K SMS/month |
| **SMS Professional** | $40/month | ~10,000 SMS | $0.008–$0.012 each (after credits) | 5K–15K SMS/month |
| **SMS Advanced** | $50/month | ~20,000 SMS | $0.006–$0.010 each (after credits) | 15K–50K SMS/month |
| **Pay-As-You-Go** | $0 setup | $0.01–$0.03 per SMS | No credits; pay per send | <1K SMS/month; trial users |

**Important**: If you send 1,000 SMS in a month via pay-as-you-go, you pay $10–$30 that month (no recurring fee). If you send 500 SMS in a month and purchased SMS Starter ($30), you paid $30 for 5,000 credits and used only 500 (credits don't roll over to next month). **You lose the unused $25 in value.**

## Part 3: Payment Processing Fees

### Stripe & PayPal Integration Costs

When you collect payments in GoHighLevel (via forms, booking, invoices, or subscription billing), GHL passes transactions through Stripe or PayPal. **Both processors charge transaction fees.**

### Payment Processing Rate: 2.2% + $0.30 per transaction

| Processor | Rate | Example ($100 charge) | Notes |
|-----------|------|------------------------|-------|
| **Stripe** | 2.2% + $0.30 | $2.50 | Paid out in 2–3 business days |
| **PayPal** | 2.2% + $0.30 | $2.50 | Paid out in 1–2 business days |
| **Stripe ACH (bank transfer)** | 0.8% + $0.30 (capped $5) | $1.10 | Slower payout; lower fee |

**These rates are current as of October 2026; verify with Stripe.com/pricing and PayPal.com/en/webapps/mpp/merchant-pricing before finalizing budgets.**

#### 3.1 Monthly Payment Processing Cost Calculator

Use this formula:

```
Monthly Processing Cost = (Monthly Revenue × 2.2%) + ($0.30 × Number of Transactions)
```

**Scenario A: Consultant charging $2,000 per client (5 clients/month)**
- Monthly revenue: $10,000
- Transactions: 5
- Processing fee: ($10,000 × 0.022) + ($0.30 × 5) = $220 + $1.50 = **$221.50/month**
- Annual: **$2,658**

**Scenario B: SaaS with $49/month subscription (200 active subscribers)**
- Monthly revenue: $9,800
- Transactions: 200 (charged monthly)
- Processing fee: ($9,800 × 0.022) + ($0.30 × 200) = $215.60 + $60 = **$275.60/month**

**Scenario C: E-Commerce shop ($50K/month, average order $75 = 667 orders)**
- Monthly revenue: $50,000
- Transactions: ~667

## Part 6: Hidden Costs & Gotchas

### Cost 1: No Credit Rollover on SMS

**The gotcha**: If you purchase SMS Starter ($30/month = 5,000 credits) but only send 500 SMS, you forfeit the unused 4,500 credits. They don't roll over.

**Mitigation**: Start with pay-as-you-go if your SMS volume is unpredictable. Switch to a monthly tier only when you consistently hit the threshold.

### Cost 2: API Rate Limiting & Custom Integration Costs

**The gotcha**: GHL's API is free, but if you exceed rate limits (typically 120 requests/minute), you may need to hire a developer to optimize your integration, adding $500–$5,000 in setup costs.

**Mitigation**: Test your integration at scale before going live. Use GHL's webhooks (more efficient than polling via API).

### Cost 3: Overage Charges on Legacy Plans

**The gotcha**: If you're still on Professional or Starter and hit contact limits (Professional is capped at 25K contacts), GHL charges overage fees (~$0.10–$0.50 per contact over the limit). Unlimited has no ceiling, so this doesn't apply if you upgrade.

**Mitigation**: Audit your contact count before signing up. If you have 10K+ contacts, Unlimited is cheaper than paying overages.

### Cost 4: Payment Processing on Subscription Billing

**The gotcha**: If you set up recurring billing (e.g., $99/month subscriptions), GHL charges processing fees on *every* renewal charge. For 100 customers on $99/month subscriptions, that's:
- 100 × $99 × 12 months = $118,800 annual revenue
- Processing: ($118,800 × 0.022) + ($0.30 × 1,200) = $2,614 + $360 = **$2,974/year** (~2.5% of revenue)

**Mitigation**: Plan for payment processing as a line item in your revenue model. Consider the impact on your margins.

### Cost 5: Dedicated Phone Number Overages

**The gotcha**: If you run voice automations with high inbound volume, overage charges for minutes can spike. For example, 1,000 inbound calls at 5 minutes each = 5,000 minutes × $0.12/min = $600 overage.

**Mitigation**: Test call volume before launching voice campaigns. Set up call routing limits to cap unexpected spikes.

### Cost 6: Missing SMS Opt-In Can Trigger TCPA Fines

**The gotcha**: If you send SMS without explicit opt-in, you can face FCC fines ($500–$43,280 per violation). This isn't a GHL cost, but it's a legal risk that affects your budget.

**Mitigation**: Always include SMS opt-in checkboxes on forms. Use GHL's SMS opt-in automations to request consent before sending promotional SMS.
