## Phase 2: Contact Overages – The Hidden Multiplier

### How Contact Overages Work

The Pro plan includes **500 contacts**. Every contact beyond 500 costs **~$1.75 each** (verified from GoHighLevel pricing API, August 2026).

**Contact overage formula**:
```
Overages = (Total Contacts - 500) × $1.75
```

### Real-World Examples

**Example 1: Agency with 1,000 contacts**
```
Included:        500 contacts (free with Pro)
Overages:        500 additional contacts × $1.75 = $875/month
Base Pro plan:   $199
Total monthly:   $1,074
```

**Example 2: Agency with 2,500 contacts**
```

```

**Example 3: Coaching business with 150 active clients**

### How to Reduce Contact Overage Costs

1. **Archive inactive contacts** (contacts with no activity in 6+ months)
   - GHL counts archived contacts but doesn't charge overages for archived records (verify current behavior; as of August 2026, archived contacts do not count toward the contact limit)
   - Archive, don't delete, to preserve history

2. **Move lost leads to a separate "archive" base**
   - Export contacts to CSV and store separately
   - Re-import when needed

3. **Consolidate duplicate contacts**
   - Merge duplicate email addresses or phone numbers
   - Reduces total count by 5–10%

4. **Upgrade to Unlimited plan** (if overages exceed $200/month)
   - Unlimited plan ($299/month) includes unlimited contacts
   - Break-even: 57+ overages = 557 total contacts
   - If you're paying $100+ in overages, Unlimited might be cheaper

## Phase 4: White-Label Domain Hosting & Custom Branding

### White-Label Domain (What It Is)

Instead of your booking link being `https://book.gohighlevel.com/your-calendar`, you can use your own domain: `https://book.youragency.com`.

**Cost to you**:
- GoHighLevel: **$0** (no additional charge for white-label domains)
- Your cost: **$12–$15/year** for domain registration (via Namecheap, GoDaddy, Route 53, etc.)

**One-time setup**: ~15 minutes (DNS configuration)

### How to Set Up White-Label Domain

1. **Buy a domain** (if you don't have one)
   - Go to Namecheap, GoDaddy, or Route 53
   - Buy a domain: `youragency.com`, `book.youragency.com`, `schedule.youragency.com`
   - Cost: $12–$15/year

2. **In GoHighLevel**, go to **Settings > Domain Management**
3. Click **+ Add Domain**
4. Enter your domain: `book.youragency.com`
5. GHL provides DNS records to add (CNAME or A record)
6. Add the DNS records to your registrar (Namecheap control panel, etc.)
7. Verify DNS propagation (can take 24–48 hours)
8. GHL generates an SSL certificate automatically

Once verified, your booking links use your custom domain.

### White-Label Branding Add-Ons (Optional, Extra Cost)

Beyond the free white-label domain, GHL offers optional branding upgrades:

| Add-On | Cost | Details |
|--------|------|---------|

| Custom email sender name/domain | Included in Pro | Free with Pro plan |
| Custom SMS sender ID | Included in Pro | Free with Pro plan (US only; some countries require phone number) |
| Custom color schemes & logos | Included in Pro | Free with Pro plan |

Don't pay for features you won't use.

Every 3 months, review your contact list and archive (don't delete) contacts inactive for 6+ months.

- Reduces total contact count by 10–30%
- Lowers overage costs by $175–$525/month (if 100–300 contacts archived)
- Archived contacts remain searchable and retain history

### 3. Consolidate SMS Channels

Instead of sending SMS from multiple platforms (Twilio + GHL + Mailchimp), consolidate into GHL.

- Reduces tool cost by $20–$50/month
- Simplifies audit trail (all SMS in one dashboard)
- Easier to track SMS ROI (open rates, conversions)

Email is included in GHL (unlimited). SMS costs $0.04/message.

- Reminder for optional webinar: Email (free)
- Appointment confirmation (same-day): SMS ($0.04, worth it for no-show prevention)

**Rule**: SMS for time-sensitive, high-value touchpoints. Email for everything else.

### 5. Negotiate Volume Discounts (Enterprise)

If you're spending >$5,000/month, contact GHL sales for custom pricing.

- Volume discounts available for 3,000+ contacts

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does GoHighLevel Pro include payment processing?

Yes. The Pro plan includes unlimited transaction processing via Stripe, Square, or PayPal. GHL charges **no transaction fees** beyond your payment processor's standard rates (Stripe: 2.2% + $0.30 per transaction; Square: 2.6% + $0.30).

### Can I upgrade or downgrade mid-month?

Yes. Upgrades are prorated (you pay the difference). Downgrades take effect next billing cycle (no refund for the current month).

### Is there a long-term contract?

No. GHL Pro is month-to-month with no long-term commitment or early termination fee. If you cancel, your account deactivates immediately.

### Are there hidden fees I should know about?

No documented hidden fees as of August 2026. The costs are:
- Base plan ($199)
- Contact overages ($1.75 per extra contact)
- SMS ($0.04 per message above the 1,000 included)
- Optional domain registration ($12–$15/year, paid to your registrar, not GHL)

Always check your invoice line-by-line to confirm.

### How does GHL pricing compare to HubSpot?

HubSpot's lowest-tier paid plan is **Sales Hub Pro** at $500/month (verified August 2026). GHL Pro is $199/month base. However, HubSpot's enterprise plan includes more advanced reporting and higher contact limits. For agencies and coaches <2,000 contacts, GHL is 2–3x cheaper. For large enterprises, prices become comparable once you factor in add-ons and custom deals.

### What if I exceed my SMS allotment by accident?

Overage SMS are charged at $0.04 per message and added to your next invoice. No surprise charges; GHL shows your SMS usage in real-time on the dashboard.

### Can I get a discount for annual billing?

Yes. **Annual billing is 20% off list price**:
- Monthly: $199/month = $2,388/year

Savings: $476/year. Downside: if you cancel mid-year, your remaining credit is typically non-refundable (verify current policy before signing up).
