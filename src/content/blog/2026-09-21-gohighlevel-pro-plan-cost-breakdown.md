You're running an agency. You've heard GoHighLevel is "cheaper than HubSpot and Pipedrive combined."

But when you look at the pricing page, you see:
- **Pro: $199/month**

Your first question: *What does each plan actually include, and which one is right for my agency?*

Your second: *At what contact volume does overage cost exceed a higher plan's flat rate?*

Your third: *How much am I really paying per contact, and does it beat Pipedrive, HubSpot, or Brevo?*

This guide answers all three. It breaks down Pro vs. Standard vs. Unlimited, shows real cost-per-contact math, and tells you when to upgrade.

> - **GoHighLevel Pro costs $199/month** and includes 500 free contacts, CRM, email, SMS, automations, landing pages, and integrations. Each contact beyond 500 costs $1.75/month. At 1,000 contacts, you pay $199 + (500 × $1.75) = $1,075/month on Pro.

> - **Unlimited plan is cost-effective at 600+ contacts**: At 600 contacts, Pro costs $299/month (base + overage); Unlimited costs $299/month with no overages. At 1,000 contacts, Pro costs $1,075 vs. Unlimited's $299 — a **$776/month difference**. Unlimited becomes cheaper immediately after the break-even point.

Every prospect, lead, customer, and contact in your GHL database counts toward the limit, including those you've imported from prior tools (Sendinblue, HubSpot, Pipedrive, etc.). Deleted contacts don't reset the count (they stay in your billing history); archiving doesn't reduce cost.
> - **Pro plan includes**: CRM with unlimited custom fields, email marketing (unlimited sends), SMS (charged separately per message, ~$0.01–$0.025/SMS depending on carrier and volume), automations (unlimited), landing pages (unlimited), calendar booking, basic integrations (Zapier, Make, webhooks), form builder, surveys. **Pro does NOT include**: white-label dashboard (Unlimited only), dedicated account manager, or priority support.

Useful for agencies reselling GHL or managing 50+ team members.

At 5 team members, budget an additional $300–$1,500/month for seats. Total cost for a 5-person agency: $199 (Pro) + $500 (avg seats) = $699/month base.

For 1,000 SMS/month, add $10–$25. SMS volume scales with client outreach (appointment reminders, promotional texts, two-factor auth); budget conservatively.

Pro: $199/month × 12 = $2,388/year, or ~$2,150/year with annual discount ≈ 10% off. Useful for budgeting, but watch for price increases mid-year if contact overage applies.
> - **Real cost per contact** (Pro, 600 contacts, 12 months): ($199 × 12) + (100 contacts × $1.75 × 12) = $2,388 + $2,100 = $4,488/year ÷ 600 contacts = **$7.48/contact/year, or $0.62/contact/month**. Pro is cheaper per contact up to ~1,400 contacts; Unlimited is cheaper per contact above that (due to flat fee eliminating overage step increases).

Brevo (Sendinblue) email ($20/month for 5,000 contacts) + Pipedrive CRM ($99/month) + separate SMS = $119/month minimum. For agencies using 4+ separate tools, GHL consolidation often saves money within 6 months.

Cost scales: 1 client costs $299; 10 clients on GHL resale costs $299 + (10 × $199–$299 client seats) = $2,289–$3,289/month total. Premium integrations (Salesforce, NetSuite, etc.) may require custom development ($500–$5,000 one-time) or a higher support tier. ## Part 2: Contact Overage Cost Explained

### How Contact Billing Works

**"Contacts" = all CRM records**, not just email subscribers. This includes:
- Prospects (leads you're pursuing)
- Customers (active clients)
- Former customers (archived or completed)
- Imported contacts from prior tools (Sendinblue, HubSpot, Pipedrive, etc.)
- Duplicates (if not merged or deleted)

**Overage cost is ongoing**, not one-time:
- Contact created = counts toward monthly limit
- Contact deleted = does NOT reduce overage cost (charge remains in that month's bill)
- Contact archived = does NOT reduce cost (it's still in your database)

**Example**: You're on Pro ($199). You have 600 contacts.

Cost = $199 + (100 contacts over limit × $1.75) = $199 + $175 = **$374/month**.

Next month, you delete 50 contacts (now 550). Cost = $199 + (50 × $1.75) = **$287.50/month** (overage reduced).

### Overage Break-Even Point: Pro vs. Unlimited

At what contact count does Unlimited become cheaper than Pro?

**Math**:
- Pro cost = $199 + (Contacts over 500 × $1.75)
- Unlimited cost = $299 (flat, no overage)
- Break-even = $199 + (X × $1.75) = $299
  - X × $1.75 = $100
  - X = 57.14 contacts
- **Break-even = 500 + 57 = 557 total contacts**

**Interpretation**: At 557 contacts, both plans cost ~$299/month. Below 557, Pro is cheaper. Above 557, Unlimited is cheaper.

## Part 4: SMS and Hidden Costs

### SMS Pricing (Not Included in Monthly Fee)

SMS is paid separately, per message sent.

**Typical rate**: $0.01–$0.025 per SMS, depending on:
- **Volume**: Higher volumes (10,000+ SMS/month) get volume discounts
- **Carrier**: Domestic (US) is cheaper; international SMS costs more
- **Content type**: Standard text is cheaper; promotional or complex messages may cost more

**Example SMS scenarios**:
- **100 SMS/month** (e.g., 20 appointment reminders + 80 promotional): ~$1–$2.50/month
- **500 SMS/month** (high-touch agency): ~$5–$12.50/month
- **2,000 SMS/month** (large-scale campaigns): ~$20–$50/month

**Calculation**: Budget **$0.01–$0.025 × expected monthly SMS volume**.

## Part 8: Pro Tips for Cost Optimization

![Part 8: Pro Tips for Cost Optimization](/images/2026-09-21-gohighlevel-pro-plan-cost-breakdown-s2.jpg)


### Tip 1: Choose Unlimited Early (Avoid Overage Shock)

Many agencies start on Pro ($199), grow to 600+ contacts, and are shocked by a $500+ monthly bill due to overage. If you plan to scale, start on Unlimited ($299) and avoid surprises. The $100/month upfront investment often pays off within 3–6 months.

### Tip 2: Merge Duplicate Contacts

Duplicates count toward your contact limit. Regularly audit for:
- Same email, different name (manual merge in GHL)
- Similar names, same phone (check for typos, merge)
- Imported contacts that overlap with existing (deduplicate on import)

Merged: 50 duplicates = remove 50 from billing = 50 × $1.75 = $87.50/month savings on Pro.

### Tip 3: Archive Inactive Contacts (if you don't need them)

GHL allows contact archiving, though archived contacts may still count toward billing (verify with support). If a contact is truly done (prospect converted, unsubscribed, dead lead), delete rather than archive to reduce contact count.

### Tip 4: Negotiate Annual Billing

GHL offers ~10% discount for annual upfront payment. For Pro at $199/month:
- Monthly: $199 × 12 = $2,388/year
- Annual: $199 × 12 × 0.90 = $2,149/year
- Savings: $239/year

For Unlimited at $299/month:
- Annual: $299 × 12 × 0.90 = $3,227/year
- Savings: $328/year

**Tip**: Ask your GHL account manager for higher discounts (15–20%) if you're on a 2–3 year commitment or large team.

### Tip 5: Use Zapier/Make for Integrations Before Custom API

Custom integrations can cost $500–$5,000. Zapier ($19–$99/month) or Make ($10–$30/month) covers 80% of use cases (contact sync, automation triggers, etc.) for 1/10th the cost.

### Tip 6: Monitor SMS Volume

SMS can creep up (reminder: 1,000 unplanned SMS = $10–$25 extra). Set SMS sending limits per contact or per campaign:
- Use GHL's built-in SMS throttle (e.g., max 5 SMS per contact per week)
- Audit SMS volume monthly (GHL reports section)
- Alert team if volume exceeds budget
